- **Technology & Tools** — pods OOM/crash-looping; upstream dependency timeout cascade; LB/ingress with zero healthy backends; app-tier or upstream pool exhaustion (*not* DB pool); TLS/sidecar failure; memory leak

- **Information** — no 503-source-attribution dashboard; logs not aggregated; misleading alert routing
