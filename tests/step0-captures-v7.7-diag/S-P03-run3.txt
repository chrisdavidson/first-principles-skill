## Effect
> Checkout API returned 503 errors for 40 minutes starting 14:10 UTC yesterday, affecting all users. Database layer ruled out.

A 503 with this shape — all-users, time-boxed, sudden onset — favors a **shared chokepoint** (capacity, dependency, or config) over a slow-burn or partial-segment cause.

## Prioritized branches (investigate in this order)

1. **Dependencies — synchronous upstream failure** (payment/auth). Cleanest single explanation for total 503s with a healthy DB. *Verify: upstream dependency dashboards, 14:10–14:50.*
2. **People/Process — change at 14:10.** Sharp onset time is the strongest correlation signal; pull the deploy/config timeline first — fastest to confirm or eliminate.
3. **Technology — LB health-check misfire.** Textbook "everything's 503 but the DB is fine." Cheap to check via target-group health history.

## Onset vs. duration
The **40-minute duration** is likely a *separate* sub-cause from the trigger — most often a **retry storm** or **slow on-call/autoscaler response** that prolonged the outage.

## Scope note
All branches are unverified candidate causes — a fishbone maps the cause *space*, it doesn't diagnose. "Database ruled out" is taken as verified ground truth, which is why capacity/connection-pool branches are scoped to *non-DB* dependencies.
