## Reasoning per option
- **PostgreSQL** — Only option with full multi-table ACID at arbitrary scope; ~$300–900/mo managed; arbitrary-query flexibility for evolving e-commerce requirements.
- **MongoDB** — Multi-document transactions supported since 4.0 but not design-optimal; its document-flexibility strength isn't what a transaction-heavy order/payment/inventory core needs. Viable but dominated.
- **DynamoDB** — Transactions capped at 100 items/4MB, 2× write cost, single-region, and demands upfront access-pattern lock-in. Rejected for this case **unless** the real requirement is multi-region active-active (Global Tables), which would flip its standing.
