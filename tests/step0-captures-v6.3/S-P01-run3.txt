### The root structural weakness
Every major failure mode traces to **two compounding decisions**:

### Top failure causes
1. **100% blast radius** — first production-only bug hits everyone at once; no canary catches it
2. **Friday-night failure, skeleton crew** — worst time to detect and recover
3. **No tested rollback / one-way door** — the most dangerous and least-discussed item; if legacy Stripe is decommissioned or drifts out of sync, you can't fall back

6. **Load collapse** — new service has never seen peak EU volume
7. **Regulatory breach** — SCA/PSD2 regression or data-residency misconfig becomes a fine, not just an incident
8. **No clean detection signal** — without a control group, a 5% success drop looks like "normal Friday variance"

### Recommended changes (priority order)
1. **Add a staged rollout** (1% → 10% → 50% → 100%) — the single structural fix; neutralizes failure modes #1, #4, #6, #8
2. **Don't launch Friday** — move to a fully-staffed Tue/Wed morning
3. **Prove rollback before cutover** — keep legacy Stripe warm and in-sync, rehearse the fallback
