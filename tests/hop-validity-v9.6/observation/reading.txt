What this reading can and cannot show
----------------------------------------
It shows whether this model, on this one fixture, grades the non-sequitur differently under the
two rubric texts. It does not show how the agent grades its own live output, which is 999.147's
job. A 3/3-vs-3/3 split at N=3 is consistent with the rubric change having an effect on this
model and this fixture; it is not statistical proof at this sample size, and it says nothing
about generalization to other fixtures, other models, or the agent's self-graded live behavior.
Do not read this split as success or failure of either rubric text.
