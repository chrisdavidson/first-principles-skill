**Criterion 4: Reason Upward**
Quoted span: "→ 20 ms of headroom means the service will stay within its SLO when traffic doubles again"
Band: **Sound**
Justification: The chain is in the correct form: the head line uses valid `GT-N (gloss)` identifiers, every hop starts with `→`, each hop fits on one line, and Abandoned Reasoning uses the What-was-tried / Why-abandoned / What-it-ruled-out structure. But the hop quoted above does not follow from the line before it. Knowing there are 20 ms of headroom today says nothing about how p99 latency changes when traffic doubles. The hop quietly assumes that latency will rise by no more than 20 ms under double the traffic. That assumption is not in the Assumptions Table and carries no inline `[Assumes: X]` tag. GT-3 is listed in the head but none of the hops actually uses it, so it does not support the assumption either. The step therefore adds no genuine intermediate inference, and the only intermediate claim that actually holds (the 200 − 180 = 20 ms headroom) cannot support the conclusion alone. That fails the Rigorous limbs for a genuine intermediate and for `[Assumes:]` declarations. No Hand-wavy trigger applies: every conclusion has a chain, every GT-ID cited exists, no analogy is used and no generic escape valve is used.
