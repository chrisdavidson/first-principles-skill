# Confidence-Transitivity Falsification Catalog — v9.4 (backlog 999.119)

## Purpose

This file is the `--probe` prompt source for Phase 42's D-07 confidence-transitivity
falsification: the pilot capture, the five-capture before leg, and the five-capture after leg.
K-of-5 is a recorded observation, never a gate, per `docs/v8.7-constraint-teardown.md` §2 item 3.

**Parser note:** `scripts/check-quality-harness.py::_read_quality_catalog` treats every line in
this file that starts with `|` as a table row and raises `ValueError` the instant one does not
belong to the `| ID | Prompt | Notes |` table below. For that reason the decision record states
its outcome classification and other structured facts as prose and bulleted lists rather than as
markdown pipe tables — no line before the `## Catalog` table's own header may start with `|`.

## Decision record

- **Capture IDs.** `CT-P1`, plus `CT-P2` only if the D-09 revision fires, are pilots and are
  excluded from K and Q. `CT-B1` to `CT-B5` are the before leg (D-10). `CT-A1` to `CT-A5` are the
  after leg (D-11).

- **Transport.**
  `ANTHROPIC_MODEL=claude-opus-5 python3 scripts/check-quality-harness.py --probe <ID> --catalog tests/confidence-transitivity-v9.4/catalog.md --plugin-dir first-principles --out <scratch>`.
  `--plugin-dir first-principles` exercises the working tree, not the plugin cache. The locked
  argv has no `--model` slot, so the pin is the recorded `ANTHROPIC_MODEL` precedent, and the
  model actually served is read from each capture's init event (D-12). Captures run one at a
  time. Only a `rate_limit_stub`, `transport_error_stub` or `no_terminal_result` capture is
  re-dispatched under the same ID. Any other outcome is kept and reported.

- **Definition of a qualifying chain (D-09, D-13).** The capture's section 4 is pairable, and the
  chain's head (as read by `_chain_head_refs`) meets all three conditions:
  - it cites at least one known chain other than itself;
  - every cited chain's stated label is exactly MEDIUM;
  - it contains no GT reference ending in `?`.

- **Definition of Q.** The number of captures in the leg that contain at least one qualifying
  chain.

- **Definition of K.** The number of those captures in which every qualifying chain has a parsed
  label of MEDIUM or LOW. A qualifying chain whose own label is unparsable blocks a yes for its
  capture. This is anti-masking, inherited from 41 D-04.

- **What else is reported.** Every capture with no qualifying chain and every capture that did not
  complete or does not resolve is counted and reported, never dropped (D-13). A supplementary
  count, `ceiling_bound_mixed`, is also reported. It counts chains whose cited chains mix MEDIUM
  or LOW with HIGH, and whose head has no `GT-N?`. It is labelled supplementary and is not part of
  K or Q, because D-09 restricts qualifying chains to heads that cite only MEDIUM chains.

- **The fixed before reading (D-15).** It stays `tests/reference-reads-v9.2.1/DEMO-TRIAGE.md`,
  with `confidence_inversions` = 1 and C8 HIGH over C3, C5 and C6.

- **Pre-registered expectation, written before any capture.** In the before leg, some qualifying
  chains may be rated HIGH, as DEMO-TRIAGE's C8 was. In the after leg, qualifying chains are
  expected to be rated MEDIUM or lower, with confidence lines that name the inherited `Cn`. A
  reading where `K_after/Q_after` is not above `K_before/Q_before`, or where `Q_after` is 0, is
  recorded as a finding against the fix's reach. It is never re-read as a pass. At N=5 the result
  is noise-limited, and that is stated.
