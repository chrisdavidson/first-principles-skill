### Six failure modes to prepare for (prioritized)

1. **Hot misalignment beyond rated envelope** *(highest priority — the canonical commissioning-window mode)* — Cold-set alignment shifts when the first hot-salt thermal gradient causes differential growth, pushing shafts past the coupling's rated offset and dumping reaction loads into bearings. → *Verify thermal-growth offsets were pre-set in the cold alignment; plan a thermal soak before first roll.*

2. **Salt freeze / thermal shock at the coupling boundary** — Fill brings salt near its freeze point; trace-heating gaps create steep local gradients and shock stress. → *Verify trace-heating coverage and warm-up ramp limits near the coupling.*

3. **Lubricant breakdown OR disc-pack fatigue** *(mutually exclusive by coupling type)* — Gear/grid couplings can run dry if hot soak exceeds grease ceiling; dry disc/diaphragm couplings can fatigue-crack suddenly under combined steady+cyclic flexure. → *Resolve by confirming coupling type first.*

4. **Guard/spacer interference + fastener relaxation** — Differential growth closes clearances and relaxes bolt preload on the first cycle. → *Check hot clearances; schedule a post-first-cycle re-torque.*

5. **Critical-speed / rotor-bow exposure on first roll** — Uneven salt-side heating bows the rotor through critical speeds. → *Enforce slow-roll soak and vibration hold points.*
