**Key insight (root pattern):** Most top failure modes share *one* structural root — **the assembly is set cold and first experiences its true thermal state at commissioning.** These are not six independent risks but one vulnerability with several expressions.

4. **Hot fastener-torque check** — re-verify coupling bolt pre-load after the first thermal cycle.
