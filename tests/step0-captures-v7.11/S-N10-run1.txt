### What it's asking for

1. **Installation #1's actuals vs. plan** — cost overruns, schedule slips, thermal-spec underperformance, what broke or surprised you in commissioning and operation. Each is a *verified* risk, not a speculative one.
2. **What's different this time** — site, salt chemistry/supplier, tank size, contractor, EPC structure, grid interconnect, climate, jurisdiction. Differences are where #1's data stops protecting you.

4. **The contract structure** — fixed-price vs. cost-plus, who owns the thermal-performance guarantee, liquidated damages, force-majeure and salt-supply clauses. (This is where "technical" failures become *your* financial failures.)
5. **Hard constraints** — commercial-operation deadline, financing covenants, offtake/PPA obligations tied to in-service date.
