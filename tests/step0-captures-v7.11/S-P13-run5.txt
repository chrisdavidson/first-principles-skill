# 50 MW Solar-plus-Storage Microgrid, Rural Sub-Saharan Africa — Cost Estimate

**All-in overnight CapEx ≈ $84M central, bracketed $64M–$119M** (~$1.3–2.4M per MW-AC). With standard remote-site contingency, budget **~$90M**.

## Key reasoning notes

- **Sizing ambiguity flagged:** "50 MW" was interpreted as AC point-of-interconnection paired with ~65 MWp DC array (1.3 DC:AC ratio). This is the **single biggest swing factor** — alternative ratios move solar cost ±$8–11M.
- **Storage:** 50 MW × 4 h = 200 MWh, costed at the global ex-US/China benchmark ($125/kWh), not US NREL figures (which embed US labor/tariffs).
- **Interconnection** is the widest relative uncertainty and the only unverified ground truth: a 30–50 km rural spur could push the total toward the upper bound on its own.

## Confidence: MEDIUM

The ~1.9× bracket is fine for financing-scale sizing but not a final investment decision. To tighten: (a) confirm the DC:AC design ratio, and (b) obtain site-specific distance-to-grid and a host-utility interconnection study.

**Explicitly excluded** (each a material additional layer): land, development/permitting fees, financing cost (notably high in SSA — roughly doubles lifetime LCOE but doesn't affect overnight CapEx), O&M, import duties/VAT, and grid-forming/islanding control equipment.
