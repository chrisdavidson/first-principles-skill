# Cost Estimate: 50 MW Solar-plus-Storage Microgrid, Rural Sub-Saharan Africa

**All-in CAPEX ≈ $165M central, bracketed $105M–$235M** (constant 2026 USD, excluding financing)

## Breakdown by scope

| Scope | Low | Central | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — PV procurement + EPC** (65 MWp DC, from 50 MWac × 1.3 ratio) | $52M | $62M | $78M |
| **B — Battery storage** (200 MWh / 4-hour) | $30M | $60M | $80M |
| **C — Interconnection civils** (substation + tie-line + site works) | $11M | $20M | $35M |
| **D — Soft costs / contingency** (12–22%) | $11M | $24M | $42M |
| **TOTAL** | **~$104M** | **~$166M** | **~$235M** |

**Per-MW sanity check:** ~$3.3M/MWac central — passes the smell test (bare US/MEA solar+4h storage runs ~$2.0–2.5M/MW; the gap is the SSA logistics/risk premium).

## Key insights from the first-principles approach

- **Two unverified, load-bearing assumptions drive the 2.2× spread:**
  1. **SSA logistics/risk uplift (+15–35%)** — hits PV, battery install, and soft costs.
  2. **Interconnection scope** — the tie-line length/voltage is the single most location-dependent factor.

- **To tighten before financing**, the agent recommends two concrete actions: (1) a site survey to pin down grid tie-in distance/voltage (collapses Factor C), and (2) a procurement RFQ for landed module + battery container costs at destination port (collapses the SSA uplift).

- **Explicitly excluded from this number:** financing/WACC (likely the *largest* lifetime cost in SSA at 12–18%), battery mid-life replacement (~year 10), O&M, land acquisition, and grid-impact study outcomes.

The estimate is grounded in IEA, IRENA, Statista, Ember, and NREL 2025 benchmarks, with each unit-factor traceable to a stated source or flagged assumption.
