**Causes** (unfiltered, from at least three stakeholder viewpoints):
- *Engineer:* Module boundaries were defined once and never enforced (no lint/ownership rules), so the monolith degenerated into a big ball of mud within a year. - *Product/end-user (who lives with the result):* A bug in an unrelated feature area caused a full-app outage/rollback that a properly isolated service would have contained. Because everything ships together, a risky feature delayed an unrelated, ready-to-ship feature's release.
- *Competitor:* A well-funded competitor adopted microservices early with a dedicated, funded platform team, and used the resulting scaling headroom and team-autonomy to out-hire and out-ship this team once both companies scaled past a much larger headcount.
