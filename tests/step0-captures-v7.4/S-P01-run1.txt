# Pre-Mortem: Payments-Rewrite EU Launch

The agent treated this as a focused pre-mortem (imagining it's already Saturday and the launch has failed, then reasoning backward).

## The two structurally fatal choices — both embedded in the plan itself
- **"Replacing" legacy** → removes reversibility. If a bug appears at hour 2, there's no warm fallback, so mean-time-to-recovery has no floor.
- **"No staged rollout"** → removes the early-warning signal. The first evidence of failure arrives from production at 100% blast radius.

## Four failure patterns that cluster
- **Pattern A — No reversibility:** big-bang + no warm legacy = unbounded recovery time. *Dominant weakness.*
- **Pattern B — No early signal:** no canary + correctness-blind monitoring = you learn late, at full scale.
- **Pattern C — Timing amplifier:** Friday-into-weekend multiplies every failure's duration (thin staffing, slow vendor/bank response).
- **Pattern D — Silent money corruption:** double-charges and reconciliation drift harm customers before dashboards show anything.

## The most likely actual Saturday-morning state
Not a clean outage, but an **ambiguous partial failure** (e.g. 92% vs 99% success rate) where no one is confident enough to pull the trigger — and hours burn in decision paralysis.

## Recommendation (HIGH confidence on direction)
**Do not ship as specified.** Three non-negotiables before any EU-wide launch:
1. **Keep legacy warm behind a kill-switch** — reversal in seconds, not a redeploy *(highest-leverage single change)*.
2. **Stage the rollout** (1% → 10% → 50% → 100%) — even hours at 1% surfaces latent defects at 1% blast radius.
3. **Move off Friday** — ship Tue/Wed with full staffing through the 72h danger window.

Plus two go/no-go gates: **prod-scale load test** and **SCA/3DS conformance pass**.
