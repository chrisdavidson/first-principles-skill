The first-principles agent ran a **pre-mortem** on the migration. Here's what it surfaced.

## The core insight
Your confidence isn't reassurance — it's the **mechanism of failure**. The belief "it'll go fine" is exactly what removes the dry runs, the field-level reconciliation, and the rehearsed rollback. The bias is the root cause feeding everything else.

## Failure modes, by phase

**Data quality** — dirty source data nobody profiled ("it's our system of record, it must be fine"), silent type truncation, encoding/timezone/null corruption, broken referential integrity from wrong table ordering.

**Completeness** — row counts match but *content* doesn't (totals reconciled, fields weren't); delta records created mid-migration on a live source get dropped; deletes reappear or active records vanish.

**Window/scale** — tested on a sample, blew the cutover window at full volume; table locks halt the business; ran out of disk/connections mid-run leaving a half-migrated state.

**Cutover/rollback** ⭐ highest priority — no *rehearsed* rollback; passed the point of no return before validation finished; a manual runbook step skipped at 2am under pressure.

**Downstream** — integrations, reports, caches, search indexes break on new schema/IDs because they were out of migration scope.

## The two structural weaknesses
1. **Confidence-induced under-testing** — the bias removes profiling, full-scale dry runs, and field-level checks.
2. **No rehearsed reverse path** — a failed migration you can cleanly reverse is an inconvenience; one you can't is an incident.

## Recommended actions (convert confidence into evidence)
1. **Full-scale dry run** at production volume, timed against the cutover window
2. **Field-level reconciliation** (checksums/hashes), not just row counts
3. **Profile the source now** — duplicates, orphan FKs, nulls, encoding
4. **Rehearse the rollback** as a timed drill with a hard go/no-go checkpoint ← top priority
5. **Inventory downstream consumers** and test the critical ones against migrated data
6. **Build incremental-delta handling** if the source stays live during migration
