**Why abandoned:** Phase 2 explicitly challenged and discarded A-3 — nothing in GT-2 through GT-5 rules out two or three moderate factors compounding, and the user's own framing of the problem flags this as a premise worth testing rather than accepting. ### Dead End: Computing an absolute dollar revenue-at-risk figure

**What was tried:** Attempted to convert the churn-rate figures (GT-2) into an absolute dollar amount of annual recurring revenue at risk, to give leadership a single headline number.

### Dead End: Ranking the six categories by industry base rates for SaaS churn causes

**What was tried:** Considered citing typical B2B SaaS industry findings (e.g., "onboarding is usually the leading churn driver in mid-market SaaS") as a prior to rank the six categories before looking at Northbrook's own two clues.

### Dead End: Assigning point-probability weights to each of the six categories
