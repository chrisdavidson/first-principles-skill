**Band (from §6):** MEDIUM

Reject the claim that a 290–565 °C solar-salt store returns more than 85% of its charging electricity (§6). With resistive charging the ceiling is 56.3% and the realistic figure is 39.6% (C2). A perfect heat pump with a 40% steam plant still tops out at 70.3% (C3). Efficiency parity alone could not make the store cost-competitive (C6). Confidence is MEDIUM because the cost question rests on the unverified GT-10? and GT-11? (C7).
