The real risk isn't the price tag, it's the causal bet: you haven't diagnosed *why* churn jumped from 4.2% to 6.8% in two quarters, and a points-for-credit perk only works if the cause is "members don't feel rewarded for loyalty" rather than something a loyalty program can't fix (price, product gaps, or the rival's launch pulling people away on features/price rather than rewards). If the driver turns out to be the latter, $310K buys you nothing and you'll still be at 6.8% next quarter.
