The closing hedge about needing city-specific data to "pin down" specifics doesn't erase the core recommendation — it sharpens the conditions of application. **Band: Rigorous.**

These are checkable, city-specific facts a reader could actually go verify, not boilerplate "more research is needed" language, and the document is explicit that obtaining them is what would pin down the recommendation rather than leaving it generic. **Band: Rigorous.**
