## Criterion 1: Decision usefulness

That recommendation is concrete enough to execute — it names an approximate target percentage, a structure, and a checkpoint horizon — and the document states plainly for whom and under what conditions it applies (this specific person's stated facts, with named exceptions where the recommendation would flip). The heavy hedging elsewhere in the document is about the certainty of the underlying numeric inputs, not about what action to take next, so it doesn't blunt the practical edge. This clears the top band.

## Criterion 2: Evidence grounding

Most of the load-bearing factual claims are genuinely well-grounded: external statistics (startup failure causes, equity dilution/exit-probability methodology, founder ownership medians, emergency-fund norms) each carry an explicit provenance tag distinguishing "read-at-source" from "reported-by-delegate," and the document is honest about two failed direct-source attempts (403/404) rather than silently upgrading their confidence. Where a headline dollar figure could mislead (the $105k/year naive equity value), the document explicitly flags it as illustrative of a methodology rather than a number to rely on. However, the pivotal artifact that actually produces the headline recommendation — the six-criterion, five-option weighted trade-off table in chain C3 — assigns 1–5 scores to each option-criterion cell with no shown derivation for why, say, "Stay" gets a Career capital score of 2 versus "Composite" getting 5. That is a real, load-bearing gap in an otherwise strong grounding record.

This meets the top band's bar of engaging with the most consequential assumption, not just listing it.

## Criterion 4: Calibration

Confidence is expressed at every level and is broadly tied to the strength of what's behind it — the document is consistent about rating every chain LOW and explains why, and it draws a genuinely useful distinction between the robustness of the general framework (don't binarize, discount headline equity, price job-search timing) and the much weaker confidence in the person-specific numeric verdict. That's a specific spot where the confidence language runs slightly ahead of the actual support.
