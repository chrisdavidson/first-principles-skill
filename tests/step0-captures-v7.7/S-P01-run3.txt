## Pre-Mortem: Payments-Rewrite Big-Bang EU Cutover

The core finding: **the risk here is structural, not incidental** — it comes from the *shape* of the plan (big-bang, all-EU, regulated, no rollback ramp), so hardening the plan matters more than hardening the code.
