## How the auth rollout could go badly — failure modes to prepare for

The agent reframed this as a **pre-mortem** (assume the rollout failed, reason backward to causes) and clustered the risks. In priority order by blast radius:

**1. No validated rollback / point-of-no-return crossed too early** *(highest priority)*
Once credential/session migration starts, any single failure becomes unrecoverable. **Fix:** require a tested rollback at every cutover and a go/no-go gate before any irreversible migration step.

**2. Unenumerated identity edge cases**
Service accounts, SSO federations, API keys, and long-lived tokens break silently if not inventoried. **Fix:** explicit identity inventory and test matrix before scheduling any team.

**3. Capacity overcommit + serial dependencies**
Teams deprioritize rollout work against their own deadlines; one team's slip propagates through a serial schedule. **Fix:** sequence team-by-team with containment between cutovers, and build the timeline around the *slowest* team, not the average.

**4. Detection-by-user, not by monitoring**
Failures get found by locked-out users instead of dashboards. **Fix:** auth-specific monitoring plus a named per-cutover owner with rollback authority *before* the first team migrates.
