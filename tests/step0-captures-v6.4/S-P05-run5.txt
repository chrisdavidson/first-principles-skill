### Why PostgreSQL
- **Correctness (top weight):** Native FKs/constraints + SERIALIZABLE isolation — best fit for inventory + order + payment committing atomically. MongoDB has ACID but only snapshot isolation; DynamoDB transactions are bounded (100 items/4 MB, no cross-index, single-region).
- **Data model:** Orders↔line-items↔products↔inventory is inherently relational with reporting joins.

### Cost reality check (500 w/s — MEDIUM confidence, list prices)

### When it flips
