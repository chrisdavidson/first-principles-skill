/first-principles:first-principles-analysis A household holds $60,000 in cash above an already-funded six-month emergency reserve, and carries a remaining $240,000 balance on a 30-year fixed-rate mortgage at a 6.25% APR, with 27 years remaining on the term. Mortgage interest is currently *not* itemised on the household's tax return (the standard deduction exceeds Schedule A's would-be total, so the marginal mortgage-interest tax shield is effectively 0%). The household's marginal federal+state income-tax rate on investment returns held in a taxable brokerage account is approximately 28% on ordinary income / 18% on long-term capital gains and qualified dividends. Two deployments are under consideration: (A) apply the full $60,000 as an additional principal payment to the mortgage, and (B) invest the full $60,000 in a broad-market index fund in a taxable brokerage account. The decision horizon is 10 years.

Analyse this from first principles. Show the arithmetic for every computed figure and recompute it.
