/first-principles:first-principles-analysis Northbrook Analytics is a B2B SaaS company offering a data-integration and reporting platform to mid-market operations teams. Contracts run $18,000–$40,000 per year and renew annually. In Q1 the quarterly churn rate was 4.1%; by the end of Q3 it had risen to 9.2% — a 124% increase over two quarters. The Customer Success team has flagged rising support-ticket volume and a drop in feature-adoption scores, but the company has not identified whether churn is driven by offer gaps, pricing pressure, onboarding failure, service quality, competitive displacement, or some combination. Leadership has instructed the team to "find the root cause of churn and fix it."

Analyse this from first principles. Show the arithmetic for every computed figure and recompute it.
