### How it was built (bottom-up, from physics)

```
1 kWh (3.6×10⁶ J) + cp of solar salt (≈1,500 J/kg·K) + ΔT (565°C hot / 290°C cold = 275K)
  → mass of salt needed per usable kWh = 3.6×10⁶ / (1,500 × 275) ≈ 8.73 kg/kWh
  (this is physically pinned, ±5% — not the uncertain part)

× blended solar-salt bulk price (≈$0.70/kg, range $0.50–$1.00)
  → salt inventory cost ≈ $6.1/kWh

× (1 + heat exchangers/pumps/piping/insulation/I&C cost-share, ≈0.6× salt cost per CSP literature)
  → non-tank total ≈ $10/kWh
```
