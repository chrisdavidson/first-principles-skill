## 4. Focused-Estimate Analysis

**Target quantity and units:** Capital cost per kWh of usable (thermal) storage capacity, excluding tanks, for a 200 MWh molten-salt system → **$/kWh_th**.

**Decomposition into unit-factors:**

```
$/kWh_th  =  (kg salt / kWh_th)  ×  ($/kg salt)              [salt cost component]
           +  ($/kWh_th, non-tank BOP: HX, pumps, insulation, controls, EPC)   [BOP component]
```

Units check: (kg/kWh_th) × ($/kg) = $/kWh_th ✓. Second term is already in target units by construction from GT-5's percentage breakdown of GT-7.

**Step 1 — Salt cost component (from GT-3, GT-4):**

- Central: 8.73 kg/kWh_th × $0.8/kg = **$6.98/kWh_th**
- Lower bound: 8.73 × $0.5 = $4.37/kWh_th
- Upper bound: 8.73 × $1.2 = $10.48/kWh_th

**Step 2 — Non-tank BOP component (from GT-5, GT-7):**

Total installed cost (tanks included), central range $20–35/kWh_th → take central $27/kWh_th.
Tanks are 30–40% of that → non-tank share is 60–70%.

- Central: 0.65 × $27/kWh_th = **$17.55/kWh_th** (this already includes the salt cost embedded in the literature figures, so Step 1 is a sub-check/cross-validation, not additive on top of Step 2 — see note below)

**Reconciling Step 1 and Step 2 (avoiding double-counting):** GT-5's 60–70% "non-tank" share already includes salt cost inside it. So the correct combination is: non-tank BOP total (from GT-7 × non-tank share) = **central $17.55/kWh_th**, bounded by:

- Lower: 0.60 × $20/kWh_th = $12/kWh_th
- Upper: 0.70 × $35/kWh_th = $24.5/kWh_th

The independently-derived salt-only figure ($7/kWh_th central, from Step 1) is used as a **sanity check**: it should be a plausible fraction (roughly 35–45%) of the non-tank total. $7 / $17.55 ≈ 40% — consistent with typical published breakdowns where salt is ~25–35% of *total* (tank-inclusive) cost and thus a larger share of the *non-tank* subtotal. This cross-check passes.

**Central estimate: ≈ $15–18/kWh of usable thermal capacity, tanks excluded.**

**Bracket: [$10/kWh_th, ~$16/kWh_th, $25/kWh_th]**

- Lower: $10/kWh × 200,000 kWh = **$2.0M**
