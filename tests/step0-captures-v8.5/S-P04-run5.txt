The first-principles agent completed the analysis. Its findings:

## Root cause (MEDIUM confidence)

**Two distinct root causes**, not one:

1. **Unbounded in-process resource accumulation** — most likely connection-pool or thread-pool exhaustion from a missing or mis-sized downstream timeout. The restart-recovers signature is the key evidence: a restart resets heap, pools, and circuit-breaker state, so a fault that clears only on restart points to accumulated state.
2. **Detection/remediation latency** — a liveness probe that understood the failure would have restarted the pod without a human in the loop.

## The five whys chain

500s → requests couldn't acquire a resource → pool exhausted by leaked/blocked connections → downstream call had no timeout (or one longer than pool checkout timeout) → resource-exhaustion bugs are structurally invisible without load testing with fault injection.

A **parallel chain** explains the 12 minutes: alerting likely thresholded on sustained error rate rather than slope, and remediation was manual.

## Caveats it flagged

- "Pod restart caused recovery" is **unverified** — a dependency could have self-healed concurrently.
- Whether a deploy/config change occurred at 09:30 is unverified; if one did, most of the analysis is void.
- "8k transactions affected" is ambiguous — retried-and-succeeded ≠ lost. That number drives customer-comms obligations.

## Three checks to raise confidence

1. Alert-fire vs. incident-start vs. restart timestamps — splits the 12 min into detect-vs-remediate.
2. Pool utilisation for the 60 min before 09:30 — a ramp confirms the primary chain; a flat line falsifies it.
3. Downstream dependency error/latency rates across the window.

It also noted a second-order risk worth carrying into any fix: shortening timeouts increases retry volume against an already-degraded downstream, so pair timeout changes with backoff-plus-jitter and a circuit breaker. And deeper liveness probes risk correlated restart storms — probes should fail readiness before liveness.

One standalone recommendation regardless of cause: **add pre-restart heap/thread dump capture to the runbook**. The restart that fixed the symptom destroyed the evidence needed to close this analysis.
