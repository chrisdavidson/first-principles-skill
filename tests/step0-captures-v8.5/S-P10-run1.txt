**Three caveats worth your attention:**

1. **Thermal vs. electrical.** The analysis assumed 200 MWh_**th**. If you meant electrical delivered, divide by ~0.40 power-block efficiency → **$110–190/kWh_e**, ~$22–38M. Biggest single swing factor.
2. **"Pre-commissioned" was read as** factory-qualified tanks arriving as a package — commissioning *labor* avoided (~10% off the tank line), hardware still in capex. If you meant the tanks are already sunk cost, drop that line: **~$28/kWh_th** (~$5.6M).
3. **Tank $/m³ is the load-bearing uncertainty.** At 200 MWh this system is small versus typical CSP TES (1,000–3,600 MWh), so it carries a scale penalty. One firm tank vendor quote would collapse most of the $30–75 range.

Confidence is medium — salt price and tank $/m³ are both unverified commodity/vendor figures. Also flagged but not costed: ~10,400 thermal cycles likely means at least one tank-floor fatigue intervention over 30 years (O&M, not capex).
