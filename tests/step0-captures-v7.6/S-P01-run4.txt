3. **SCA/3DS2 regression as a compliance event.** EU-specific and legal (PSD2/SCA), so its cost is categorically worse than a generic outage — not just lost revenue. (Confidence: HIGH)

5. **No pre-agreed abort threshold or business-metric monitoring.** Failure gets debated instead of acted on; dashboards watch system health (latency, 5xx) rather than authorization success rate vs. legacy baseline.
