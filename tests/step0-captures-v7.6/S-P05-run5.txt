## Why PostgreSQL wins
The two highest-weighted criteria converge on it: **native, default-grain ACID transactions** (critical for atomic order + inventory + payment writes) *and* the **lowest cost band** at ~$260–$700/mo against your $3k ceiling — 4–10× headroom. JSONB columns also absorb document-shaped catalog data, neutralizing the main reason to reach for MongoDB.

## Why the others ranked lower
- **DynamoDB (last):** Strongest on raw throughput, but loses on what e-commerce weights most — constrained transactions (100-item cap, 2× write cost, single-region), highest cost here (~$1,600–$2,600/mo), and unpredictable per-request billing under spikes.
- **MongoDB (second):** ACID exists since v4.0 but is off the engine's default grain; beaten on every weighted axis.
