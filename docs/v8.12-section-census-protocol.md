rewrite of the rules that follow — the same discipline `docs/v8.12-corpus-triage-protocol.md`
demonstrates for the corpus-triage pre-registration this document mirrors in shape.

**Authored blind.** No document in `~/fp-corpus-v8.12/analyses/`, in
`tests/quality-baseline-v8.7-postfix/analyses/`, or in `tests/quality-baseline-v8.10-oos/analyses/`

## Purpose and scope

This protocol governs three things: the ANCHOR-03 control census, the ANCHOR-01 real-corpus census,
and the ANCHOR-02 verdict. **The same instrument runs on both corpora.** A rule amended between the
control census and the real census would void the comparison — that is the exact hazard ANCHOR-03
exists to prevent, and it is why every rule below is fixed here, once, before either census starts.

## Section unit (D-05)

A heading is **ATX only**: a line whose first non-whitespace characters are one or more `#`
followed by whitespace. Content inside fenced code blocks is never a heading, regardless of what it
contains.

Per document, the **census level** is the shallowest ATX heading level that carries any prescribed
role. Every heading at that level, in document order, is a section, numbered 1-based. Headings at
deeper levels are sub-structure, not sections. If no level carries any prescribed role, the census
level is `none` and the document tiers ROLE-ABSENT.

**Forcing reason, stated on the record.** The control corpus already mixes `#` and `##` for the
same six roles — some documents carry the template at `##`, others at `#`. A fixed-`##` rule would
report all six roles absent on a `#`-level document. That is a measurement artifact, not a finding,
and the level-agnostic rule above exists specifically to make that artifact structurally impossible
rather than something a reviewer has to catch after the fact.

If any document uses setext-style headings (an underline of `=` or `-` beneath a text line, rather
than a leading `#`), that is recorded as an anomaly in the census record and resolved by hand — it
is never silently absorbed into either heading form.

## Role mapping (D-06)

**Normalization procedure**, applied to every candidate heading before any matching:

1. Trim leading and trailing whitespace.
2. Strip a leading ordinal prefix: an optional section sign, digits with optional dotted
   sub-numbers, and an optional trailing period, parenthesis, colon, hyphen, or dash.
3. Strip a leading `Phase <digits>` prefix together with its separator.
4. Strip surrounding markdown emphasis characters (`*`, `_`, backtick).
5. Strip trailing punctuation.
6. Lowercase.
7. Collapse internal whitespace to single spaces.

**Pass 1 — exact match.** The normalized title is compared for exact equality against the six
normalized template titles: `problem essence`, `assumptions table`, `ground truths`,
`derivation chains`, `abandoned reasoning`, `conclusion`. A match assigns that role and pass 2 does
not run for that heading.

**Pass 2 — closed lettered judgment rule**, applied only to headings pass 1 does not match. Every
pass-2 application is recorded per document, by its letter:

- **(a) Containment** — the normalized title contains exactly one of the six template titles as a
  whole-word substring, and no other template title. That role is assigned.
- **(b) Fixed synonym table** — published here, extended only by a recorded dated amendment, never
  silently mid-census:
  - conclusion ← `recommendation`, `recommendations`, `final recommendation`,
    `summary and recommendation`, `verdict`
  - derivation chains ← `derivation`, `derivations`, `chains`, `reasoning chains`
  - ground truths ← `facts`, `verified facts`
  - assumptions table ← `assumptions`
  - abandoned reasoning ← `dead ends`, `abandoned paths`, `discarded reasoning`
  - problem essence ← `problem`, `core problem`, `essence`
- **(c) Bounded body inspection** — permitted **only** for a candidate for the derivation-chains
  role, and only to look for the named structural markers `GT-`, the rightwards-arrow chain glyph,
  or a chain-label heading of the documented `C`-plus-digit form (e.g. `C1`, `C2`). **No body
  inspection of any kind is permitted for a conclusion candidate** — an unresolved conclusion
  candidate is recorded as unlocatable, never resolved by reading its body. Every clause-(c)
  inspection is logged with its document, heading position, and the marker found; the total count
  performed across the whole census is published.
- **(d) No number-based assignment, ever** — the stripped ordinal prefix from normalization step 2
  is never used to assign a role. Using the number to identify the role assumes the answer to the
  question this phase asks.

**Disclosed asymmetry (the cost of option A, stated as the checkpoint required).** Clause (c) gives
a derivation-chains candidate one extra chance to be located that a conclusion candidate does not
get, because reading conclusion body text is exactly what the blindness boundary below forbids. This
asymmetry can only push a document **toward** ROLE-ABSENT — it can never resolve a document away
from ROLE-ABSENT that clause (c) was not applied to. The reason blindness beats completeness here:
this phase must stay blind to what conclusions say, so the census accepts an asymmetric
location-success rate between the two roles rather than compromise that boundary.

## Tier vocabulary (D-03)

- **CONFORMING** — Derivation Chains sits at position 4 **and** Conclusion sits at position 6.
- **RENUMBERED** — both roles are located, and at least one sits at a position other than its
  prescribed one.
- **ROLE-ABSENT** — at least one of the two roles cannot be located at all.

ROLE-ABSENT is a distinct tier, deliberately, so that "the numbering moved" and "the document has no
conclusion" can never collapse into the same result.

**Adjacency cases, fixed in advance.**

- Two headings at the census level sharing a normalized title are separate sections at distinct
  positions; if both map to the same role, that role takes the first occurrence in document order,
  and the duplication is recorded as a judgment call.
- A heading whose normalized title contains two distinct template titles is recorded as
  `merged-role`, assigned to both roles at that single shared position, and flagged.
- Two roles resolving to the same position is permitted, cannot tier CONFORMING (because positions 4
  and 6 differ), and therefore tiers RENUMBERED and is flagged.

**Empty cases, fixed in advance.**

- A document with no ATX headings at all has census level `none`, section count 0, tier
  ROLE-ABSENT, and the note `no-headings`.
- A document with exactly one section at the census level is censused normally under the rules
  above.
- No document is ever dropped from its corpus for being empty — an empty document is a ROLE-ABSENT
  finding, not a missing row.

## Category vocabulary (D-07)

Seven fixed tags for non-role (inserted) sections, published here and extended only by a recorded
dated amendment, never silently mid-census:

| Tag | Definition |
|-----|------------|
| `document-title` | The document's own title, where it sits at the census level. |
| `process-output` | Process/meta commentary about how the analysis was produced (e.g. a closure ledger). |
| `scope-note` | A note bounding what the document does or does not cover. |
| `missing-inputs` | A note flagging inputs the analysis lacked. |
| `validation-record` | A record of a validation or review pass over the analysis. |
| `domain-content` | Anything client- or project-specific, absorbed here without being described further. |
| `other` | Any inserted section none of the above six tags fit. |

Inserted sections are recorded as **position plus tag, never verbatim heading text** — identically
on both corpora. `domain-content` absorbs anything client-specific without describing it, so no real
heading text ever leaves the working set.

**Why `document-title` exists as its own tag.** A document whose own title sits at the census level
would otherwise be counted as an unnamed inserted section and silently shift every position that
follows it. Naming the tag lets the census publish a pre-registered sensitivity figure — how many
documents would change tier if `document-title` sections were excluded — instead of burying the one
judgment most likely to swing a result inside an undifferentiated `other` bucket.

**Why the same rule binds the control.** Categorising real headings by tag while quoting harness
headings verbatim would stop the two censuses being the same instrument — the exact hazard ANCHOR-03
exists to prevent. The category rule therefore applies identically, with no verbatim heading text
recorded from either corpus.

## Version recording (D-02)

Each document's agent-body version, or generation date where version is not directly recorded, is
recorded on both corpora. **No rate is stratified by version.** Reason: the real corpus spans
roughly five agent-body versions across 11 in-denominator documents, which gives cells of one to two
documents each — no rate computed at that resolution would be meaningful. The version is recorded
so a reader can see the generational spread and judge whether a real-vs-harness difference could be
a version artifact, not so it can be used to slice a rate.

## Statistics and precision contract

Per corpus, define:

- `k` = documents tiered RENUMBERED
- `a` = documents tiered ROLE-ABSENT
- `L` = documents tiered CONFORMING or RENUMBERED

**Precision contract.** Every rate is reported as its raw integer `k/n` pair. A decimal or
percentage is a derived convenience shown to at most one decimal place using **round-half-to-even**,
and is never stated without its `k/n` pair alongside it. No clause of the decision rule below is
ever evaluated on a rounded value — every decision comparison in this protocol is between integers,
so no rounding can move a verdict.

**Two pre-registered secondary statistics**, explicitly **not** part of the verdict cut:

- The **relative-order figure** — how many documents have their located roles in the template's
  relative order among themselves, ignoring inserted sections.
- The **`document-title` sensitivity figure** — how many documents would change tier if every
  `document-title`-tagged section were removed before numbering.

## Verdict decision rule (ANCHOR-02)

Stated as integer comparisons over the **real corpus alone**:

- `L = 0` → **NOT-WELL-FORMED**, ground `ROLE-ABSENCE`.
- Otherwise: `k <= 1` → **WELL-FORMED**, ground `POSITIONS-HOLD`.
- Otherwise (`k >= 2`) → **NOT-WELL-FORMED**, ground `RENUMBERING`.

**The boundary, stated explicitly:**

- `k = 0` → WELL-FORMED
- `k = 1` → WELL-FORMED (at the cut)
- `k = 2` → NOT-WELL-FORMED (one step above the cut)

**Rationale for the cut, fixed in advance.** A single exception can be named and handled as a named
exception inside a mechanical coding rule. Two or more means the exception is the pattern, not the
exception.

Both verdicts — WELL-FORMED and NOT-WELL-FORMED — are complete results. Neither is phrased as the
phase succeeding and the other as the phase failing. No figure will be re-derived, no rule
re-worded, and no threshold moved after any census figure exists.

## What the control contributes

**Attribution vocabulary**, fixed in advance against the **control's own `k`**:

- Control `k = 0` → `REAL-USE-SPECIFIC`
- Control `k = 1` → `INDETERMINATE` (a single instance at n=12 cannot distinguish real-use-specific
  from pre-existing)
- Control `k >= 2` → `PRE-EXISTING`

**Instrument-validity clause.** If the control's `k` plus `a` is 1 or more, the record states that
the harness corpus every prior positional measurement ran on itself contains documents where
positional anchoring mis-locates — and records that as a limitation on those **prior** measurements,
never as evidence about real use.

**Attribution never changes the verdict.** The verdict is decided on real output alone, per the
Verdict decision rule above; the control cannot be used to launder a real-corpus finding. The
verdict statement is incomplete unless both the real corpus's and the control's `k/L` pairs appear
side by side.

## What is published under each verdict (SC-4)

**NOT-WELL-FORMED** publishes the role-location procedure above, restated as an application
procedure a reader could run by hand on one document: which role identifies the conclusion, which
identifies the derivation chains, and how each is located when the numbering differs from §4/§6 —
plus a disposition for documents where a role cannot be located at all (coded not-codeable under
whatever anchoring survives, reported separately, never silently dropped from Phase 180's
denominator).

**WELL-FORMED** records that Phase 180 anchors positionally on §4/§6; the role-absence disposition
above still applies to any document where a role cannot be located.

## Blindness boundary (SC-5)

The census reads heading level, position, and normalized title only. The working-set helper never
renders body text. Body inspection occurs only under pass-2 clause (c), only for a
derivation-chains candidate, only for the named structural markers — every instance is logged and
the total published. **No conclusion body text is read anywhere in this phase**, so the Phase-180
pre-registration stays blind to what the conclusions say.

## Privacy rule

No verbatim heading text from either corpus enters any artifact — not the tracked findings document,
not the working-set census record, not a commit message, not a plan summary. Real-corpus documents
are named by label plus chronological index; control documents are named by baseline filename. The
working-set census record carries roles and category tags, never titles.

## What this protocol does not assert

At commit time, no census figure exists, no tier count exists, no category-tag count exists, and no
verdict exists. This document records rules only.
