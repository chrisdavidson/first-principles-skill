# v8.10 — Out-of-Sample Validation Pre-Registration Protocol (FIX-CONTRACT-01)

**This document is pre-registered and immutable.** It is authored and committed together with
`tests/quality-catalog-v8.10-oos.md` in a single commit — the pre-registration anchor — **before**
any of the ~6 fresh analyses named in that catalog is generated, captured, or opened. Once
committed, this protocol is never edited to match a later hand-read result, detector run, or
disposition. Any amendment discovered to be necessary after the read begins is recorded as a new,
dated addendum appended below the original text, never as a silent rewrite of the rules that
follow — matching the discipline `docs/v8.9-diagnose-contract-fix.md`'s own commit history
demonstrates (its DIAG-01 section landed alone, in its own commit, before DIAG-02 existed).

**Authored blind.** Nothing under any future `tests/quality-baseline-v8.10-oos/analyses/` directory
has been opened, and no fresh analysis has been generated, while writing this protocol. This
document records rules only. It contains no per-document classification, no per-document detector
number, and no verdict — all three are produced downstream, after this commit, strictly by applying
the rules fixed here.

## Purpose

Phase 173 exists specifically to supply the out-of-sample control the v8.9 `DIAGNOSE-01` diagnosis
never had. v8.9 resolved its single most consequential reading fork — whether a "conclusion claim"
subject to §6→§4 tracing includes imperative/restatement/corollary sentences or excludes them —
**post-hoc**, after the read, and disclosed this itself as a departure from pre-registration
discipline ("this reading is not a formality — it is the single most consequential adjudication in
this diagnosis... the entire verdict rides on this one scope call," `docs/v8.9-diagnose-contract-fix.md`
DIAG-04). Silently inheriting that post-hoc choice here — even by reusing the same reading without
re-declaring it as a choice — would reproduce the exact failure v8.9 flagged in itself. This protocol
therefore fixes, **before** any fresh analysis is read: what evidence counts, how it is classified,
what reading position is applied, and how the per-document and aggregate verdicts are computed —
so the eventual finding cannot be shaped after the fact to fit a preferred story (the v8.7 lesson:
"pre-register thresholds ahead of results; label post-hoc rules post-hoc").

## The frozen problem set

This protocol governs the hand-read and detector run of exactly the six fresh analyses generated
from the six rows (`Q-N1`..`Q-N6`) of `tests/quality-catalog-v8.10-oos.md`, the fixed and
pre-registered A1 problem set — one fresh analysis per row (repeat=1, six topic-distinct problems,
per the Phase 173 plan's A1 decision). No other analyses are in scope for this protocol.

## Adapted evidence, form, and detector-judging criteria

The following three criteria sets are adapted, by explicit reference, from
`docs/v8.9-diagnose-contract-fix.md`'s DIAG-01 section (D-03(a), D-03(b), D-03(c)) — reproduced here
verbatim in substance so this document is self-contained and independently readable, without
requiring the reader to cross-reference v8.9 mid-read.

### Adapted D-03(a): Evidence criteria — what counts as an attempted §6→§4 closure ledger

A fresh analysis document is judged to have **attempted** a section-6 (Conclusion) to section-4
(Derivation Chains / Ground Truths) closure ledger for a given conclusion claim if the claim's
sentence or bullet in section 6 contains **at least one** of the following, appearing in the claim's
own text (not merely present elsewhere in the document):

1. A chain identifier that also appears as a chain label inside section 4 (e.g. a token of the form
   `chain C` followed by a number, or an equivalent bracketed/parenthetical chain tag used
   consistently by that document's section 4 — including the current `### Conclusion C1: [text]`
   numbering convention now in force per `shared/spine/references/output-template.md` §4, which did
   not exist when the six frozen v8.7-postfix analyses were generated).
2. Two or more ground-truth identifiers (however that document labels ground truths — numbered,
   lettered, or named) that also co-occur together inside a single section-4 chain block.
3. Prose language that explicitly points backward at section 4 as the claim's justification (e.g.
   "as shown in the chain above," "per the ground truths cited in §4," "following from the
   derivation in section 4") even where no bare identifier is repeated, provided the referenced
   section-4 content is locatable and actually supports the claim.

A claim with **none** of the above is judged **not attempted** — a bare assertion with no traceable
link back to section 4, regardless of how confidently it is worded.

### Adapted D-03(b): Form taxonomy — recognized attempt forms

Every attempted closure ledger recognized under (a) falls into exactly one of three forms:

1. **Explicit ledger table** — the claim sits inside (or is immediately followed by) a Markdown
   table row, or an explicit bulleted "claim → chain/ground-truth" mapping list, whose
   columns/structure exist specifically to pair conclusion claims with their section-4 support.
2. **Inline per-claim citation** — the claim's own sentence or bullet directly embeds a chain
   identifier or ≥2 co-occurring ground-truth identifiers (per adapted D-03(a) criteria 1–2), with
   no separate table or ledger structure — the citation travels with the claim itself.
3. **Prose cross-reference** — the claim's own sentence uses backward-pointing natural-language
   phrasing (per adapted D-03(a) criterion 3) that names or clearly locates the supporting
   section-4 content, without a bare identifier token and without a table structure.

A claim judged "not attempted" under (a) is classified as **no form** (none of the three applies).

### Adapted D-03(c): Detector-judging criteria — correct vs false-positive

For a given flagged claim (one `check-quality-harness.py`'s `detect_defects()` counts into
`untraced_claims`), the detector's verdict is judged, against the adapted D-03(a)/(b) criteria above
and the actual claim text:

- **CORRECT** — the claim, read under the adapted D-03(a) evidence criteria, has **no** traceable
  link back to section 4 in any of the three adapted D-03(b) forms. The detector and the hand-read
  agree: the claim genuinely lacks a §6→§4 trace.
- **FALSE-POSITIVE** — the claim **does** satisfy at least one adapted D-03(a) criterion (a competent
  reader classifies it into one of the three adapted D-03(b) forms), but `_claim_is_traced` still
  returned false for it — a format the detector cannot recognize, or a single ground-truth
  identifier that legitimately supports the claim but falls under the ≥2-co-occurrence threshold.

This is a per-claim judgment, not a per-document one. FIX-CONTRACT-01 (quick task 260724-bq3)
already corrected three specific detector limitations (bare-letter chain-label families, citation
normalization, extraction-scope filtering) — a fresh false-positive found here that is **not** one
of those three already-fixed patterns is itself a new detector-limitation finding to record, not
silently folded into the existing three.

## Both reading positions — pre-declared, applied in parallel

v8.9's own text names the unresolved scope question precisely: D-03(a) defines evidence criteria
for a claim **once one exists**, but never defines what counts as a "conclusion claim" in the first
place. Two readings exist, both already written up in `docs/v8.9-diagnose-contract-fix.md` DIAG-04:

1. **`declarative-only`** — a "conclusion claim" subject to the tracing requirement is a
   declarative, evidence-bearing assertion that purports to rest on section 4 — not an imperative
   next-step instruction, a section-intro label, a restatement of an already-cited verdict, or a
   logical corollary entailed by adjacent already-cited claims. This is the reading v8.9 adopted,
   but only **after** its own read (post-hoc).
2. **`every-extracted-sentence`** — every sentence `_conclusion_claims()`'s mechanical extraction
   produces counts identically as a conclusion claim, with no mood/form exclusion, regardless of
   whether it is imperative, a label, a restatement, or a corollary.

**Pre-declared position (fixed now, before any fresh analysis is read): this protocol applies BOTH
the `declarative-only` reading AND the `every-extracted-sentence` reading in parallel to every one
of the six fresh analyses.** Each fresh document therefore receives two independent per-document
verdicts (one per reading) and the aggregate D-01 arithmetic (below) is computed twice — once per
reading — rather than the hand-read committing to a single reading up front.

**Why both, not one:** (1) this is the direct out-of-sample sensitivity re-test the v8.9 diagnosis
never had against fresh evidence — reporting only one reading here would silently repeat v8.9's own
scope gap rather than closing it; (2) Phase 174 (`CGATE-05`, design-only, out of this phase's scope)
is where the reading ambiguity itself gets resolved as an instrument-design problem, and a two-reading
result gives Phase 174 a concrete, fresh sensitivity table to design against, mirroring exactly the
kind of divergence v8.9 already demonstrated on the frozen six; (3) declaring a single reading here —
whichever one — cannot be justified as anything other than either "silently inherit v8.9's post-hoc
choice" or "assert a new post-hoc choice of our own," both of which this protocol exists to avoid.

## Per-document verdict: traced / untraced / partial

Under **each** reading independently, every fresh document's conclusion claims (as extracted and
filtered per that reading's own scope) reduce to exactly one of three per-document verdicts:

- **TRACED** — every conclusion claim in the document (under the active reading) is traced, per
  adapted D-03(a)/(b)/(c): either the claim was never flagged by the detector, or every flagged
  claim on this document is judged FALSE-POSITIVE under adapted D-03(c).
- **UNTRACED** — the document's central recommendation, or a majority of its conclusion claims
  (under the active reading), is genuinely untraced — i.e. judged CORRECT (genuinely unlinked) under
  adapted D-03(c).
- **PARTIAL** — at least one conclusion claim traces (per adapted D-03(a)/(b)) and at least one
  conclusion claim is genuinely untraced (judged CORRECT under adapted D-03(c)), and neither
  condition for UNTRACED (central recommendation or majority genuinely untraced) is met.

This three-way partition is a refinement of v8.9's binary mechanism-signal/measurement-signal
reduction (adapted D-02, below) — PARTIAL exists here specifically to give a fresh document an
honest middle disposition rather than forcing every mixed-evidence case into one of the two poles,
matching v8.9's own observation that a "partial-trace document" is the pivotal case its own D-02 rule
had to resolve one way or the other.

### Adapted D-02: Per-doc reduction rule — fixed before the read

Mapping the three-way TRACED/UNTRACED/PARTIAL verdict onto v8.9's mechanism-signal/measurement-signal
vocabulary for the D-01 aggregate arithmetic below:

- A document verdicted **TRACED** is **measurement-signal** for that reading (every flagged claim,
  if any, is a false-positive; the agent's own reasoning fully closed the §6→§4 loop).
- A document verdicted **UNTRACED** is **mechanism-signal** for that reading (the document's central
  recommendation or a majority of its conclusion claims genuinely fail to trace — the agent failed to
  produce the closure the contract requires).
- A document verdicted **PARTIAL** is also **mechanism-signal** for that reading, matching v8.9's own
  D-02 rule exactly: "a single genuinely-untraced claim tips that document to mechanism-signal,
  regardless of how many other claims in the same document trace cleanly." PARTIAL is a richer label
  for the hand-read record than v8.9 carried, but it reduces to the same conservative pole for the
  aggregate arithmetic — one genuinely-untraced claim is never averaged away.

### Adapted D-01: Verdict rule (majority-with-mixed-band) — fixed before the read

For **each reading independently**, the six per-document signals (mechanism-signal or
measurement-signal, per the reduction above) are aggregated:

- **≥ 4 of 6** docs classified measurement-signal (i.e. TRACED) → aggregate = **MEASUREMENT**.
- **≥ 4 of 6** docs classified mechanism-signal (i.e. UNTRACED or PARTIAL) → aggregate =
  **MECHANISM**.
- Anything else (including a clean 3/3 split, or any distribution reaching neither threshold) →
  aggregate = **MIXED**.

This rule is one-way per reading: once the hand-read begins, it cannot be revised without becoming
a post-hoc rule dressed as a pre-registered one. Whatever the twelve classifications (six documents
× two readings) turn out to be, this threshold arithmetic is what computes each reading's aggregate —
no discretionary override.

## The FIX-CONTRACT-01 decision gate — fixed before the read

The gate is evaluated per reading, then reconciled across both readings:

- **Fresh analyses trace** (a reading's aggregate = MEASUREMENT, i.e. ≥4/6 fully TRACED under that
  reading) → **FIX-CONTRACT-01 stands** under that reading. The out-of-sample evidence confirms the
  v8.9 MEASUREMENT verdict was not an artifact of the frozen six alone, and the offline detector fix
  already shipped (quick task 260724-bq3) required no further mechanism-level revisit.
- **Fresh analyses do not trace** (a reading's aggregate = MECHANISM, i.e. ≥4/6 UNTRACED-or-PARTIAL
  under that reading) → **FIX-CONTRACT-01 must-revisit** under that reading. The out-of-sample
  evidence indicates the v8.9 verdict was MECHANISM all along (or has become so on fresh evidence),
  and a detector-only fix was shipped against a symptom rather than the underlying agent behavior —
  this is a MEASUREMENT-signal outcome for the *fix*, not the analyses.
- **A reading's own aggregate is MIXED** (neither threshold reached) → that reading yields neither
  disposition cleanly; this is recorded honestly as an inconclusive result for that reading, not
  forced into either pole, and carried forward exactly as stated.

**If the two readings yield different dispositions** (one reading says stands, the other says
must-revisit, or either reading lands MIXED while the other does not) — **that divergence is itself
the DIVERGE finding** this milestone (`CORRECTGATE-01`) exists to characterize: a reading-dependent
verdict on fresh, out-of-sample evidence, not merely on the frozen six v8.9 already measured. This
divergence (if it occurs) is reported honestly in the Plan 03 disposition document and carried
forward as input to Phase 174 (`CGATE-04`/`CGATE-05`, design-only) — it is not adjudicated away by
this protocol, and this protocol does not pre-select which reading Phase 173 itself should trust
more. Phase 173's job is to measure and report both readings' verdicts faithfully; resolving which
reading the correctness instrument should ultimately adopt is explicitly Phase 174's province.

## What this protocol does not assert

This protocol asserts nothing about what any of the six fresh analyses named in
`tests/quality-catalog-v8.10-oos.md` will actually say, trace, or score once generated. It contains
no per-document detector number (no `untraced_claims`/`conclusion_claims` ratio, no `untraced_flag`
value) for any fresh document, because none of the six fresh analyses exists yet at the time this
document is committed. Every rule above is stated in the abstract, to be applied mechanically once
the fresh evidence exists — exactly the discipline this document exists to enforce on itself.

## Downstream procedure this protocol governs

For Plan 02 (CGATE-01): generate the six fresh analyses live (cap-defensive, background, `--out`
under `/tmp`, `--probe` per row against `tests/quality-catalog-v8.10-oos.md`), extract each via
`extract_agent_analysis()`, and freeze them under a new git-tracked `tests/quality-baseline-v8.10-oos/`
directory once capture is confirmed complete.

For Plan 03 (CGATE-02/CGATE-03): read each of the six fresh analyses in full, twice — once under
each reading — and for every conclusion claim in scope under that reading, apply adapted D-03(a) to
decide whether an attempt exists, adapted D-03(b) to classify its form if so, and adapted D-03(c) to
judge the detector's verdict (once the detector has been run) correct or false-positive for that
claim — quoting the claim text and any section-4 evidence relied on. Reduce each document to one
TRACED/UNTRACED/PARTIAL verdict per reading, per adapted D-02. Apply adapted D-01 to compute each
reading's aggregate. Apply the decision gate above to reach the FIX-CONTRACT-01 disposition (or the
DIVERGE finding, if the two readings disagree). Run `check-quality-harness.py`'s detector
(`--detect-defects` / `detect_defects()`) on the same six fresh analyses, independently of the hand
read having already been recorded, and reconcile hand-read and detector agreement/disagreement per
document, matching Phase 171's DIAG-02/DIAG-03 method. Commit the single disposition to
`docs/v8.10-fix-contract-oos-validation.md`.
