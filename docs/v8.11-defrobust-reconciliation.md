## How to read this document

For each of the four fork-sensitive documents (`Q-N2`, `Q-N3`, `Q-N4`, `Q-N6`), this reconciliation
records all **three D-05 signals**:

- **(a) raw load-bearing-claim-set overlap** — before applying any equivalence rule, what each read
  actually identified as load-bearing;
- **(b) verdict agreement** — do the two reads assign the same TRACED / UNTRACED / PARTIAL verdict;
- **(c) the binary gate verdict under D-04** — are the two claim-sets materially equivalent (a
  verdict-flipping difference is divergence; an excluded-under-D-03 or verdict-non-flipping
  difference is agreement).

Signal (c) decides the per-document reconciliation (AGREE / DIVERGE). Any case where the two reads
land on the **same verdict via a genuinely different denominator** is disclosed as an **explicit
caveat**, never folded silently into "agree" (D-05). The designer's original v8.10 reading is
reported at the end of each section, and again in a dedicated section below, **only as a third data
point — it does not enter the gate** (D-06).

**(a) Raw load-bearing-claim-set overlap.** Both reads identified the **same 10 claims**, quoted
near-verbatim from `Q-N2`'s §6 conclusion, in the same order: the "stage it" opening line, the
35kg-oversizing claim (C1), the no-permanent-double-shift claim (C5), the do-not-hold-off claim
(C2/C3), the partial-second-shift claim (C2/C4/C7), the 30-day concentration-number claim with the

pre-register-the-trigger claim (C7/C4), and the cost-is-not-the-variable claim (C3). Neither read
excludes any of these ten from the denominator, and neither adds a claim the other omits. Read-A
does not state an explicit exclusion note; read-B explicitly excludes the "What to do, in order:"
section-intro label and the Phase-5 confidence line, matching read-A's silent omission of both. **Raw
overlap: full (10/10, identical set).**

**(b) Verdict agreement: DISAGREE.** Read-A: **PARTIAL**. Read-B: **TRACED**.

**(c) D-04 gate.** The divergence is not a denominator difference — the two reads counted the
identical 10 claims. It is a difference in the **traced/untraced judgment applied to one of those
ten identical claims**: the 30-day "get the one number that decides this" claim, specifically its
quantitative sub-part (the ~20% / ~35% thresholds).

- Read-A: *"Traced to GT-16? … and A-4 … But C1's arithmetic derives 2.3x headroom and 4.6 years — it
  never derives ~20% or ~35%. The claim cites 'Rough thresholds from C1's arithmetic'; no §4 chain
  contains that computation. **Untraced on the quantitative part.**"* — this single claim is why
  read-A's document verdict is PARTIAL.
- Read-B: *"traced. §4 C1's arithmetic (2.33x headroom vs. `ln(1.2)` growth) plus GT-16? explicitly
  named as the unknown; §3 GT-16? and A-4 ('direction-changing')."* — read-B does not treat the
  specific 20%/35% figures as requiring their own derivation chain; citing C1's general arithmetic
  plus GT-16?/A-4 as the named unknown is sufficient in read-B's judgment.

Per protocol Rule (5): *"A difference that flips a document's verdict counts as divergence, regardless
of how small the differing claim-set is."* Since the denominator is identical and the verdicts
differ, this is not a near-miss the D-04 exclusion categories can rescue (it is not a section-intro
label, a restatement, or a caveat field on either side) — it is a substantive interpretive
disagreement about whether "rough thresholds from C1's arithmetic" is adequately grounded when the
specific numbers do not appear in a named §4 chain. **Gate verdict: DIVERGE.**

**Reconciliation: DIVERGE.** No caveat category applies (this is not a same-verdict case) — the
divergence is recorded directly as the gate outcome.

**Third data point (D-06, does not enter the gate):** the designer's own v8.10 reading classifies
`Q-N2` as TRACED — matching read-B, not read-A. The designer's argument (`docs/v8.10-correctness-instrument-design.md`,
"Defining the load-bearing claim") addresses a different claim than the one the two blind reads
diverged on: it argues the *opening "stage it" line* is a section-intro label and the *"pre-register
the trigger" line* is an imperative, neither load-bearing — it does not discuss the 20%/35%
thresholds claim at all. So the designer's reading is silent on the exact locus of the blind-read
divergence; the match with read-B is a same-verdict coincidence, not confirmation of read-B's
specific reasoning.

all traced, including this one) agree. **Gate verdict: AGREE** (materially equivalent under D-04).

**D-05 caveat (mandatory disclosure).** This is a genuine **same-verdict / different-denominator**
case and is disclosed here explicitly rather than folded silently into "agree": the two reads
disagree on whether the "What would change this" paragraph belongs in the load-bearing-claim set at
all. That the disagreement happens not to flip the verdict is what D-04 requires for the gate to read
AGREE — it is not evidence the denominator question itself is settled.

vs. TRACED). **Gate verdict: DIVERGE.**

**Tally: 2 of 4 documents AGREE (`Q-N3`, `Q-N4`); 2 of 4 documents DIVERGE (`Q-N2`, `Q-N6`).**

Per protocol Rule (4): *"Any residual divergence on any of the four documents … → the definition only
relocated the fork rather than dissolving it → `CGATE-BUILD-01` is marked WON'T-DO, and the DIVERGE
line is closed as characterized-but-not-closable."* The gate requires agreement on **all four**
documents; it does not. This is a conjunction, order-independent, and a single divergence is already
sufficient — here there are two.

### Disposition: `CGATE-BUILD-01` — **WON'T-DO**

The DIVERGE line first measured in `docs/v8.10-fix-contract-oos-validation.md` and targeted by the
D-03 load-bearing-claim definition in `docs/v8.10-correctness-instrument-design.md` is closed as
**characterized-but-not-closable**. Two independent, mutually-blind readers applying the identical
pre-registered D-03 rule to the identical four documents reproduced the *same load-bearing-claim
denominator* on three of four documents (`Q-N2` full, `Q-N4` full, `Q-N6` full; `Q-N3` near-full with
one disclosed caveat) — the denominator itself is close to fork-robust. But on two of the four
documents (`Q-N2`, `Q-N6`), the two readers reached **different traced/untraced verdicts** on the
identical denominator, both times over the same kind of case: a claim that cites a named §4 chain or
ground truth only indirectly or partially (a general arithmetic reference without the specific derived
number, in `Q-N2`; a claim the source document itself flags as outside its own model, in `Q-N6`).
The D-03 definition did not relocate the fork into "which sentences count" (the denominators mostly
agree) — it relocated the fork one level deeper, into **"how directly must a load-bearing claim's own
number be derived before it counts as traced."** That is a real, reproducible, unresolved ambiguity,
not a coding error in either read and not a defect this reconciliation can adjudicate away — the
protocol's D-04 material-equivalence rule exists precisely to catch this kind of verdict-flipping
difference and mark it divergence rather than paper over it.

Per honesty-not-score (D-01, protocol Rule 4 note): this is not a failure of the test — it is the test
succeeding at exactly what it was built to do. `CGATE-BUILD-01` (build and wire the correctness
instrument into the firewall battery against the v8.10 fixture manifest) is **not** worth committing
as a future build milestone on this evidence. The successor milestone remains explicitly out of scope
of this phase and is not further scoped here.
