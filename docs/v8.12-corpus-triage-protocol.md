# v8.12 — CORPUS-02 Short-Document Triage Pre-Registration

**This document is pre-registered and immutable.** It is authored and committed alone, in its own
dedicated commit — the pre-registration anchor — strictly **before** either of the two governed
documents named below is opened. Once committed, this protocol is never edited to match what a
later read finds. Any amendment discovered necessary afterwards is recorded as a new, dated
addendum appended below the original text, never as a silent rewrite of the rules that follow — the
same discipline `docs/v8.11-defrobust-protocol.md` and `docs/v8.10-fix-contract-oos-protocol.md`
demonstrate.

**Authored blind.** Neither governed document's content has been read while writing this protocol,
and none of the other eleven corpus documents has been opened at all. This document records rules
only.

## Purpose

Fix the criteria that decide whether each of the two shortest corpus documents is a full analysis,
an aborted run, or a follow-up dispatch — and thereby fix the denominator every later rate in v8.12
depends on — before either document is opened.

## Scope — the two documents this protocol governs

This protocol governs exactly the two documents in the v8.12 corpus with the smallest
extracted-analysis character counts, identified by **rank**, not by a threshold value. At scoping,
those two measured **2,271** and **1,428** characters against a **16,000–79,000** character range
for the other eleven. That measurement motivated the rank rule; it is not itself a cutoff — a
document is governed by this protocol because it ranks among the two smallest, not because it falls
below some fixed number.

The counting unit is fixed as **decoded Unicode code points** — the same unit the working-set
manifest uses — never bytes, so the identity of "the two smallest" cannot change with the counting
unit.

If a third document's extracted character count ties or sits adjacent to the second-smallest at
extraction time, the rank rule still selects exactly two documents, and the adjacency is recorded in
the triage record rather than resolved silently.

The two governed documents are listed, in the triage record, in **ascending working-set id** order,
so the record stays reproducible even where the two counts compare equal. The two documents are
named in the triage record by their working-set ids only (the label plus chronological-index
scheme); those ids are bound in the working-set manifest, and the manifest is not a tracked
artifact.

## Classification criteria

Three structural signals are fixed, verbatim, before either document is opened:

- **Signal 1 — section roles.** Which of the six prescribed template section roles the document
  carries — Problem Essence, Assumptions Table, Ground Truths, Derivation Chains, Abandoned
  Reasoning, Conclusion — counted by role **presence**, never by position. Position is Phase 179's
  question, not this protocol's.
- **Signal 2 — termination.** Whether the document ends in a conclusion-role section or stops
  mid-section.
- **Signal 3 — dispatch-prompt shape.** Whether the dispatch prompt reads as a narrow follow-up
  depending on an earlier run's output, or as a fresh problem statement.

These are structural signals only. No judgment about analysis quality, correctness or
persuasiveness is admitted — that is what keeps the Phase 179/180 blindness intact.

## Decision rule

The mapping from the three signals onto the three permitted classifications —
`full-analysis`, `aborted-run`, `follow-up-dispatch` — is fixed before any evidence is seen:

- **`aborted-run`** when the document does not terminate in a conclusion role (Signal 2 negative),
  whatever the other two signals say.
- **`follow-up-dispatch`** when the document does terminate in a conclusion role but carries fewer
  than the six section roles AND its dispatch prompt reads as a narrow follow-up.
- **`full-analysis`** when the document terminates in a conclusion role, carries all six section
  roles, and its dispatch prompt states a fresh problem.

**Residual tiebreak.** Any signal combination matching none of the three definitions above is
decided by Signal 2 alone: terminates in a conclusion role → `full-analysis`; otherwise →
`aborted-run`. The disagreement between signals is then recorded verbatim in the triage record as a
disclosed caveat. A rule that fails to decide cleanly is evidence, not an error to paper over — but
the call must still be made explicitly, following the v8.10/v8.11 reason for pre-registering a
tiebreak.

## Denominator rule

A document classified `full-analysis` counts in the denominator. A document classified
`aborted-run` or `follow-up-dispatch` sits outside the denominator but stays in the working set and
in the manifest with its classification recorded, and Phase 180 may still report it separately. The
denominator is 13 minus the number of governed documents that triage out — so 13, 12, or 11.

All three outcomes are complete answers. No outcome is chased to a different result: "both are full
analyses, denominator stays 13" and "both triage out, denominator is 11" are equally complete
answers. The criterion is that the call is made explicitly, not that it comes out a particular way.

## Blindness boundary

Only the two governed documents may be opened, and only to read section presence, termination
behaviour, and dispatch-prompt shape. Conclusion content is not read. The other eleven corpus
documents are not opened at all in this phase. Phases 179 and 180 must remain blind to the
traceability question, and this protocol does not narrow that.

## Fidelity precondition

The extraction-fidelity check — per-document assistant-text-block count and the
extracted-versus-total character ratio, plus an independent re-derivation of both governed
documents against their raw transcripts — must be recorded and resolved **before** any
classification is made. A document that is short because the extractor dropped text is a bug; a
document that is short because the run stopped early is a finding. The two must not be conflated,
because conflating them corrupts the denominator and every rate downstream.

## What this protocol does not assert

At commit time, no classification exists, no denominator value exists, and no verdict about the
untraced-conclusions question exists. This protocol records rules only. It asserts nothing about the
other eleven documents.
