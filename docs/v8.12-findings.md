## What the milestone asked, and what it found

This milestone asked whether the untraced-conclusions defect that five prior milestones measured
only on harness-generated analyses of harness-chosen problems also appears in first-principles
output produced on real external work, never previously read back. Either verdict below alone
under-reports what was found; the two are stated together.

**The traceability answer — PRESENT.** Applying the pre-registered coding rule to the codeable
subset of the real corpus, [v8.12-coding-findings.md](v8.12-coding-findings.md) returns
**PRESENT**: the untraced-conclusions defect appears at 10 / 10 over the codeable subset and
10 / 11 over the fixed eleven-document denominator (`n11`), against the re-coded control's own
9 / 12 — so the attribution is **PRE-EXISTING**, not real-use-specific. A bare `10 / 10` on its
own is not this milestone's finding; the paired figure is.

**The anchoring verdict — NOT-WELL-FORMED, ground RENUMBERING.** Applying the pre-registered
census rule to the same real corpus, [v8.12-section-census-findings.md](v8.12-section-census-findings.md)
returns **NOT-WELL-FORMED**, ground **RENUMBERING**: the real corpus's own `k`/`L` figures are
3 / 10, against the control's own 8 / 12, and the attribution is again **PRE-EXISTING** — the
measurement harness itself renumbers *more*, not less, than real work does.

**The shared denominator.** Both verdicts above share the same eleven-document denominator, fixed
by the Phase-178 corpus triage at 11 of the 13 documents originally assembled
([v8.12-corpus-triage-protocol.md](v8.12-corpus-triage-protocol.md)), cited here and not
re-derived.

## The pairing rule

This record states the pairing rule explicitly, in its own words, as a rule governing the whole
document rather than one finding inside it: **a real-corpus incidence figure may never be cited
without the re-coded control figure, and the PRE-EXISTING attribution, standing beside it in the
same sentence.** Every restatement above and below obeys it.

The rule is promoted here from a local assertion inside
[v8.12-coding-findings.md](v8.12-coding-findings.md) to a milestone-level rule for one reason: a
rule that lives inside one findings document does not survive being copied onward by a reader —
or an agent — who never saw the discussion that produced it. A bare, unpaired incidence figure is
exactly what gets quoted forward, and this project has already withdrawn one published finding to
a mismatched comparison. Stating the rule here, at the level of the record a reader actually
indexes from, is what is meant to stop that from happening a second time.

## Instrument dependence — a result in its own right

The re-coded control's post-fix six-document subset came out 4 / 6 untraced where the historical
frozen detector reported 6 / 6 on the same six documents and the same denominator
([v8.12-coding-findings.md](v8.12-coding-findings.md)). Measured incidence depends materially on
the instrument that measured it — a finding that bears on every incidence figure this project has
published, not only this milestone's own, which is why it is stated here as a result in its own
right rather than folded into the answer above as a footnote.

This delta is published **unreconciled, by design**. The pre-registration barred fitting an
explanation to it before either arm was coded, precisely so that an explanation could not be fitted
only once it would be convenient. This closure does not quietly discharge that bar: no account is
offered here for why the re-coded instrument found two fewer untraced documents than the frozen
detector did on the identical six documents. None is fitted.

## Limitations, stated not glossed

**The digest gap.** The claim that the identical instrument coded both arms rests on a SHA-256
digest lock asserted between the control run and the real run; that digest value, and the record
that it was checked and matched, both live outside this repository
([v8.12-coding-findings.md](v8.12-coding-findings.md)). Phase 180 itself names this the more
load-bearing of its limitations, because the control comparison above is void if the instrument
was not in fact identical across both arms, and a reader has no independent way to confirm that
from this repository alone.

**The exposure crossing.** During the real-corpus coding, one in-denominator document was opened
directly after its `NOT-CODEABLE` disposition had already been established from the census alone,
and approximately thirty lines of its prose were displayed before the read was stopped. The read
was unnecessary — the document had no claim set to adjudicate — but no content from it was
transcribed, quoted, or copied into any artifact, and the coded value did not change. It is
disclosed here, not only in the working-set record, because the crossing is disclosed rather than
left where it happened.

**Sample concentration.** 8 of the 11 in-denominator documents come from a single labelled
project, so the `10 / 10` and `10 / 11` headline figures above — which stand against the control's
own 9 / 12, attribution **PRE-EXISTING** — are not ten or eleven independent confirmations of the
same finding; they are heavily shaped by one project's house style. This is disclosed as a
limitation on how the figures above should be read, not as a change to the figures themselves.

## The whole-milestone privacy sweep

A whole-milestone privacy sweep across Phases 178-181 was run at close, rather than trusted from
per-phase discipline, because a leak on a public repository is permanent and retroactive. This
section restates that sweep's own record; it does not re-run it.

**What was scanned.** Every tracked file changed in the range `v8.11..HEAD` (10 files, 1,817 diff
lines) and every commit message in that same range (16 commits, 313 lines), read in full and not
sampled. `.planning/` and the outside-git working set were excluded by design: gitignored content
never reaches the public surface, so a hit there would be a discipline note, not a leak.

**How.** A two-stage method. Stage 1 was a mechanical pre-filter across six pattern categories —
named as categories only, with no pattern member ever written to disk — including a mandatory
live-hazard member drawn from this project's own planning record. Stage 2 was a full hand-read of
the complete diff content and the complete commit-message set against the operative content test
this project's use-journal already defines: would this identify the client to someone who knows
the sector? The hand-read is what discharges the sweep; the mechanical pass is the cheap net, and
a zero-candidate mechanical pass did not shorten it.

**By whom.** The human orchestrator, in the main session. No subagent was dispatched at any point
during the sweep, because the pattern list itself contains material that must not enter an
executor context, and two executor privacy incidents already occurred earlier in this milestone
under briefs that looked safely bounded at the time. Containment here is by construction, not by
care.

**The verdict — CLEAN.** No leak was found on either surface. The mandatory live-hazard member
returned zero occurrences across the entire tracked diff and all sixteen commit messages. Every
other mechanical candidate reached a not-a-leak disposition on inspection, and the independent
hand-read confirmed across all files and all messages: no raw analysis text, no verbatim claim or
heading text from either corpus, and every corpus document referred to by label plus chronological
index only. Publishing the clean case, at the same level of detail a non-clean verdict would carry,
is what makes this claim checkable rather than merely asserted.

## What this document does not claim

No judgment is made here about whether any analysis's reasoning is correct — correctness has no
instrument in this project; v8.10 designed one and v8.11 closed it, and this milestone codes
traceability structure only. No fix is proposed or applied anywhere in this document; byte-freeze
holds this milestone, and a fix, if one is ever pursued, would need its own trigger and its own
pre-registered success criterion — the PRE-EXISTING attribution above means this evidence does not
by itself establish that one is warranted. `CGATE-BUILD-01` stays **WON'T-DO** and is not reopened
by anything found here.

No successor milestone is scoped or named here, and no terminal state is declared. v8.11 was
declared terminal and v8.12 opened anyway on new evidence; that assertion has already been
falsified once, and this document does not repeat it.
