## 1. What this record establishes

This record establishes, from git-tracked dated evidence, whether the `8.14.0` version stamp
actually reaches an installed session through two distinct delivery mechanisms — the fresh-install
path and the existing-install update path. Per D-07 these are two different mechanisms with two
possibly different answers, and both are published side by side here, never collapsed into one
headline. Both answers are published whichever way each falls: "the stamp reached installs fine
and nothing was ever undeliverable" is itself a passing result for DELIV-01, and simultaneously
the exact condition that fires this record's own STOP branch in §2 below — the admissibility
anchor for Phases 189-191 depends on finding a real hole, not on this milestone's preference for
one.
