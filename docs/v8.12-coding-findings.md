## Verdict

**The pre-registered rule, stated as integer comparisons on `u`:**

- `u = 0` → `ABSENT`
- `u = 1` → `PRESENT` (at the cut)
- `u = 2` → `PRESENT` (one step above the cut)

**Observed: `u = 10`** — eight further integer steps above the cut than the boundary case itself.

Applying the rule: `u = 10`, and `10 >= 1`, so the rule yields **`PRESENT`**. The incidence is the
D-14 pair stated above: 10 / 10 over the codeable subset, 10 / 11 over `n11`.

Every comparison in this rule was between integers — `u` against 0, `u` against 1, `u` against 2. No
rounding operation of any kind entered any clause of the decision rule, so no rounding could have
moved this verdict.

**Honesty-not-score, stated as its own statement, not a hedge.** Both outcomes of this rule are
complete results; neither branch is phrased as the phase succeeding and the other as failing. The
verdict here is `PRESENT`: the untraced-conclusions defect that five milestones measured on
harness-generated analyses of harness-chosen problems also appears, at this incidence, in output
produced on real external work. No fix is proposed here. Byte-freeze holds this milestone, and a fix
— if one is ever pursued — needs its own trigger and its own milestone, on evidence this document
records but does not act on.

## Limitations, stated not glossed

**Author-auditable, not reader-auditable.** The per-claim rows and the corpus itself live outside git
because the corpus is client work; this document carries aggregates and the per-document table only.
A reader can check every arithmetic step shown above but cannot re-derive it from the underlying rows.
This is the first milestone in this project's history with that limitation.

**The identical-instrument claim carries the same limitation, and it is the more load-bearing one.**
The coding helper was digest-locked (SHA-256) between the control coding and the real coding, and the
working-set record states that the digest was re-checked and matched before the real coding began.
That digest value, and the assertion that it was checked, both live outside git — a reader cannot
confirm from this repository alone that the instrument was unmodified between the two arms. This
matters more than the per-document limitation above, because the same-instrument property is the
entire premise the control arm exists to protect: if the two corpora were not coded by the identical
rule, the comparison in "The control arm and the historical figure" above is void, and a reader has no
independent way to check that it was not.

**Clause-(c) narrowness.** The pre-registered clause-(c) exclusion shape set is closed and mechanical
— exactly two shapes, `c-restatement` and `c-corollary` — and therefore under-excludes relative to a
human reading of what counts as a near-paraphrase restatement. The count of such under-exclusion
cases (`CLAUSE-C-SHAPE-MISS`) is published above and was 0 on this corpus; the narrowness itself
remains a standing limitation on the instrument regardless of whether it was exercised this time.

**Sample concentration.** The 11 in-denominator real documents are not 11 independent observations.
Their distribution across the four labelled projects is 8 / 1 / 1 / 1 — eight of the eleven documents
come from a single labelled project, and the per-project breakdown above shows that project supplying
8 of the 10 codeable, `UNTRACED`-coded documents. The `10/10` and `10/11` headline figures should be
read with that concentration in mind — they are not ten or eleven independent confirmations of the
same finding. This is disclosed as a limitation on how the figures above are read, not as a change to
the figures themselves.

**A blindness-bound crossing, disclosed rather than left in the working set.** During the
real-corpus coding, one in-denominator document was opened directly with a reading tool after its
`NOT-CODEABLE` / ROLE-ABSENT disposition had already been established from the Phase 179 census
alone. The read was therefore unnecessary and exceeded the protocol's stated exposure bound, which
limits on-screen exposure to the single claim under adjudication — and this was not an adjudication
at all, since a `NOT-CODEABLE` document has no claim set to adjudicate. Approximately thirty lines
of that document's prose were displayed before the read was stopped. No content from it was
transcribed, quoted, paraphrased or copied into any artifact, and the coded value did not change:
the document was `NOT-CODEABLE` on the census evidence either way. It is recorded in full in the
working-set record's divergence log and is the sole entry in its exposure log. This is disclosed
here, and not only in the working set, because the protocol page above makes a public claim about
how exposure was bounded — a record that contradicts that claim must be reachable by a reader of
this repository, not live only in a file outside git.
