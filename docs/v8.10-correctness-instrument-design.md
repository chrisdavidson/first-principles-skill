# v8.10 — Design of the Correctness Instrument (design-only)

This is a **design-only specification** (CGATE-04 / CGATE-05). It fixes, on paper, what a
correctness instrument for this project measures — the property that separates a *correct* analysis
from a *well-formed wrong* one — and the acceptance criteria a future build must satisfy. **No
detector, script, or mechanical check is built here, and nothing under `shared/` or
`first-principles/` is edited.** The eventual implementation is the named downstream successor
`CGATE-BUILD-01`, explicitly out of scope (see the closing sections).

The v8.7 lesson governs the sequencing: a fix on an unvalidated theory is measured-*false* (v8.7
Phase 166 shipped a prompt-level closure instruction that left the primary defect flat, 6/6 → 6/6 —
`docs/v8.7-post-fix-remeasure.md`). So this milestone fixes the design and states falsification
criteria against already-measured cases *before* any build, so that `CGATE-BUILD-01` can be
measured-*true* rather than measured-*false*.

The project today has two quality surfaces — a structural rubric and a set of marker/trace
detectors — and **no correctness gate**. This document names what that gate measures beyond both.

## The measured property — support adequacy of load-bearing claims

The property a correctness gate measures is the **support adequacy of the analysis's load-bearing
claims**. A well-formed wrong analysis is precisely one whose load-bearing claims are well-*formed*
but inadequately *supported*; a correct one is one whose load-bearing claims each rest on support
that actually holds.

A load-bearing claim is **correct-supported** if and only if its support path is
**present ∧ valid ∧ non-vacuous**:

- **present** — the section-6 (Conclusion) claim traces to a section-4 ground-truth or derivation
  chain: the support the claim rests on actually exists in the document and is locatable from the
  claim's own text. A claim whose supporting derivation is nowhere cited fails here.
- **valid** — the supporting step is actually *true*: the arithmetic re-derives from its own stated
  inputs, the cited quantity is right, the causal link holds. A claim can be **present but false** —
  it names a chain, and the chain's math is wrong — and such a claim fails validity even though it
  passes presence.
- **non-vacuous** — the check that establishes presence or validity actually *discriminates*: it
  *could* fail on a bad input. A check that reports green because it cannot fail — because the thing
  it was supposed to test is absent, disabled, or unreachable — establishes nothing, and a claim
  "supported" only by such a check is not genuinely supported.

The instrument's score is the fraction of load-bearing claims that meet all three conditions:

> **score = |load-bearing claims that are present ∧ valid ∧ non-vacuous| / |load-bearing claims|**

The precise boundary of what counts as a *load-bearing claim* — the denominator of that score, and
the thing the whole instrument is applied to — is defined below in **Defining the load-bearing
claim**. That definition is doing real work: it is what dissolves the reading ambiguity the earlier
diagnoses left open, and it is deferred to its own section rather than assumed here. For this
section it is enough that a load-bearing claim is one of the recommendations, decisions,
quantitative results, or causal assertions the conclusion actually rests on.

## One instrument, three failure modes of support

The DIVERGE class was defined by three separately measured instances (v8.7–v8.9). They look like
three different bugs that would each need their own detector. They are not. They are **the three
ways a single property — support adequacy — fails**, one per sub-check of the triad above:

| DIVERGE instance (measured) | How support fails | Triad sub-check violated |
|---|---|---|
| reading-dependent untraced-§6 (v8.9 / Phase 173) | support is **missing** | **present** |
| rubric ≠ correctness (v8.7 P162) | support is present but **invalid** | **valid** |
| META-Q4 vacuous-green (v8.8) | the support check is **vacuous** | **non-vacuous** |

- **Missing support → the `present` sub-check.** In the Phase-173 out-of-sample evidence
  (`docs/v8.10-fix-contract-oos-validation.md`), `Q-N4`'s central recommendation — "Use the 3PL. Do
  not build the warehouse. Do not split 50/50." — plainly rests on the document's own §4 cost
  chains, yet its §6 text cites no chain identifier at all: the support is not traceable from the
  claim. This is the missing-support failure, and it is exactly what a `present` check polices.
- **Invalid support → the `valid` sub-check.** In the v8.7 P162 spot-check
  (`docs/v8.7-correctness-spot-check.md`), the `condA-P3` document PASSED the rubric while half its
  checkable load-bearing claims were arithmetically wrong (3 of 6), and the `condA-P2` document
  FAILED the rubric while its arithmetic was flawless (0 wrong). The support was *present* — the
  chains were cited — but *false*. Presence is satisfied and correctness still fails; only a
  `valid` check catches it.
- **Vacuous support → the `non-vacuous` sub-check.** The v8.8 META-Q4 case is a requirement tiered
  `reproducible` on the strength of a hook that no longer checks anything — green because nothing
  checks it. A support check that cannot fail is not evidence of support; only a `non-vacuous` check
  — one that confirms the check itself could have failed — distinguishes real green from vacuous
  green.

Because all three measured instances are failure modes of the *same* property, **one instrument that
measures support adequacy covers all three** — it does not need one detector per instance. That is
the substance of CGATE-04's "one instrument, not three detectors": the unification is not a
packaging convenience, it is the observation that missing, invalid, and vacuous are the complete
enumeration of the ways a claim's support path can be inadequate.

## What the rubric and the marker detectors already measure — and why neither is correctness

The instrument's contribution is defined by what it measures **beyond** the two surfaces the project
already has. Neither of those surfaces measures correctness, and each misses it for a structural
reason worth stating precisely.

**The rubric** (`shared/spine/references/validation-rubric.md`, run as the blind A/B judge inside
`scripts/check-quality-harness.py`) scores structural conformance to the six-section output
contract, on reproducible and quoted structural evidence. By its own design it never re-derives a
chain's arithmetic — no criterion asks a judge to redo the analysis's own math — so it is
architecturally blind to whether a number is right. This is not a tuning gap; it is a category
boundary. The v8.7 join measured it directly: the single most arithmetically accurate document in
the corpus (`condA-P2`, 100% correct) FAILED the rubric, and the least accurate (`condA-P3`, 50%
correct) PASSED it, with two documents at identical accuracy splitting one PASS and one FAIL
(`docs/v8.7-correctness-spot-check.md`; the standing "rubric conformance does not predict
correctness" limit is recorded in `docs/v8.7-quality-baseline-freeze.md` under "Honest limits").
The rubric is a formatting checker; it maps onto the `valid` sub-check's *absence*, not its
presence.

**The marker / trace detector** (`_conclusion_claims()` and `_claim_is_traced()` in
`scripts/check-quality-harness.py`) mechanically checks the structural *presence* of a citation
token — does a §6 claim name a §4 chain id or co-occurring ground-truth ids? This is a working
prototype of the `present` sub-check, and the instrument reuses its behavior as a mechanical assist.
But it does two things it cannot do: it cannot judge whether the cited support is *true*
(validity is beyond token-matching), and its own extraction can itself be incomplete or unreachable
in ways that make a green reading vacuous (the Phase-173 findings document several). Markers locate a
citation; they do not weigh it.

The instrument therefore lives in the gap both surfaces leave: **support adequacy = present**
(partly mechanized by the existing detector) **∧ valid** (which neither existing surface tests at
all) **∧ non-vacuous** (which neither existing surface guards). The remaining sections fix the
instrument's nature (which sub-check is mechanical and which is human-arbitered), define the
load-bearing claim precisely, and commit the acceptance/falsification manifest.

## Instrument nature — hybrid, human-arbitered validity

The instrument is a **hybrid**: two of its three sub-checks are mechanical, and the third — the one
that actually decides correctness — is arbitrated by a structured human hand-read. The division is
not arbitrary; it follows from which sub-checks an automated surface has already been shown able (or
unable) to judge.

- **present → mechanical.** The structural path check already has a working prototype in
  `scripts/check-quality-harness.py`: `_conclusion_claims()` extracts the section-6 claims and
  `_claim_is_traced()` decides whether each names a section-4 chain id (exact, or a normalized
  abbreviated/pluralized citation of it) or two-or-more ground-truth ids that co-occur inside one
  chain block. This is a presence test — does the citation token exist and point somewhere in §4 —
  and a machine does it well.
- **non-vacuous → mechanical / definitional.** Whether a check *could* fail is a property of the
  check itself, decidable structurally: a gate wired to an absent or disabled hook, an assertion over
  an empty set, a predicate no input can falsify. This is detectable without judging the analysis's
  content, so it is mechanical.
- **valid → structured human hand-read protocol is the authoritative arbiter.** Whether a cited
  chain's arithmetic re-derives, whether a cited fact is actually true, whether a causal step holds —
  this is the correctness question, and its arbiter of record is a structured human hand-read. The
  Phase-173 blind hand-read in `docs/v8.10-fix-contract-oos-validation.md`, run under the
  pre-registered protocol `docs/v8.10-fix-contract-oos-protocol.md` (evidence criteria fixed before
  the read, quoted excerpts, per-claim verdicts), is the working prototype of that arbiter.

**Hard rule: mechanical outputs are assists that can never override the human verdict on validity.**
A mechanical validity aid — a future arithmetic recomputation, say — may surface a candidate error
for the human to confirm, but it cannot by itself pass or fail a claim's validity. The rationale is
the measured record, not caution for its own sake: both existing automated surfaces already failed
on *truth*. The marker detector (`_claim_is_traced`) only pattern-matches citation tokens; it has no
mechanism to judge whether the chain it points at is arithmetically right. The rubric LLM-judge went
further and actively *passed* the worst-arithmetic document in the v8.7 corpus (`condA-P3`, 50%
accurate) while *failing* the flawless one (`condA-P2`, 100% accurate). Handing validity to an
automated judge would re-run the exact v8.7 measured-false trap; so validity is arbitered by the
structured human protocol, and the machine assists it rather than replacing it.

## Defining the load-bearing claim — resolving the reading fork

CGATE-05 requires the spec to **resolve, not inherit**, the untraced-§6 reading ambiguity. That
ambiguity is real and measured: v8.9 named the fork — does a "conclusion claim" subject to §6→§4
tracing include imperative/label/restatement/corollary sentences (`every-extracted-sentence`) or
only declarative evidence-bearing verdicts (`declarative-only`)? — and resolved it **post-hoc**,
disclosing that its entire verdict rode on that one scope call
(`docs/v8.9-diagnose-contract-fix.md`, DIAG-04). Phase 173 then pre-registered *both* readings and
reproduced the fragility on fresh, out-of-sample evidence: under `declarative-only` the aggregate is
4/6 measurement (FIX-CONTRACT-01 stands); under `every-extracted-sentence` it is 5/6 mechanism
(must-revisit). Those two dispositions disagree — that reading-dependent disagreement *is* the
DIVERGE finding (`docs/v8.10-fix-contract-oos-validation.md`).

The instrument does **not** resolve this by choosing `declarative-only` or `every-extracted-sentence`.
It resolves it by **defining the measured unit** — the load-bearing claim — which is the denominator
of the D-01 score and a principled third definition the triad already implies:

- **Include** (a claim is load-bearing if it is one of these, and the conclusion rests on it):
  **recommendations, decisions, quantitative results, and causal assertions** the conclusion rests
  on.
- **Exclude** (never load-bearing, so never in the denominator): **section-intro labels**,
  **restatements of already-cited content**, and the **`**Confidence:**` / caveat field**.

This definition **dissolves the fork** rather than picking a side. The documents that flipped between
the two readings — `Q-N2`, `Q-N3`, `Q-N6` — flipped for exactly one reason: a section-intro label or
a closing restatement lacked a citation *of its own*, while everything it introduced or restated was
itself cited. Under the definition those sentences are not load-bearing, so they leave the
denominator and the flip disappears:

- `Q-N2`: "Do none of the three things as posed. Stage it." is a section-intro label synthesizing
  three separately-cited paragraphs; "Pre-register the trigger now..." is an imperative next-step.
  Neither is load-bearing.
- `Q-N3`: "Recommendation: Option 3 — structured deferral..." is a section-intro label over four
  numbered, separately-cited action items.
- `Q-N6`: "The single sentence:..." is a closing restatement of a capture-rate finding already cited
  via `(chain C1)` earlier in the same section.

Crucially, the definition does **not** launder `Q-N4`'s real gap. `Q-N4` is UNTRACED under *both*
readings, and it stays UNTRACED under the definition: its crux — "Use the 3PL. Do not build the
warehouse. Do not split 50/50." together with "The decisive number:...", "The gap is not close.",
and "The split is the worst of the three." — are genuine recommendation and quantitative-result
claims that plainly rest on the document's own §4 cost chains, yet none cites a chain id (only an
assumption-table id `A-2`, which is neither a chain nor a ground-truth id). Those claims *are*
load-bearing by the definition, and they are genuinely uncited, so `Q-N4` fails the `present`
sub-check. The definition isolates the real gap instead of averaging it away.

This is the "conclusion-claim scope boundary" v8.9 flagged as the single most consequential
unresolved adjudication in the whole line of work. Resolving it is not a matter of adopting a
reading — it *is* defining the unit the instrument measures, which is CGATE-05's "resolve, not
inherit" met head-on.
