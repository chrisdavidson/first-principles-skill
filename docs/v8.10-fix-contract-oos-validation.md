### Methodological supplement (disclosed, not silently applied)

`docs/v8.10-fix-contract-oos-protocol.md` fixes the evidence/form/detector-judging criteria
(adapted D-03(a)/(b)/(c)), the per-doc reduction rule (adapted D-02), the verdict rule (adapted
D-01), and the two reading positions (`declarative-only` / `every-extracted-sentence`) — but it does
not, and could not without inventing detail beyond v8.9's own text, fix the **granularity** of a
"claim" (sentence-within-a-paragraph vs. the whole bold-lead paragraph/bullet as one unit), nor
whether the required `**Confidence:** [...]` field (a D-07 caveat-naming obligation, not itself an
evidence-bearing conclusion) is in scope for tracing at all. Two supplementary calls were necessary
and are disclosed here, before any detector output exists, applied identically across all six
documents and both readings:

1. **Claim-unit granularity: paragraph/bullet, not sentence-within-paragraph.** `173-RESEARCH.md`
   Finding 1 states `_conclusion_claims()` "mechanically extracts any bold-colon-lead-in or
   list-item **line**" (quoting `docs/v8.9-diagnose-contract-fix.md` DIAG-03). A markdown paragraph
   with no internal line break is one such "line." This hand-read therefore treats each bold-lead
   paragraph and each numbered/bulleted list item in a document's `6. Conclusion` section as **one**
   claim unit; if a qualifying citation (chain id, ≥2 co-occurring GT ids in one chain block, or
   backward-pointing prose) appears **anywhere** in that unit's text, the whole unit is judged
   traced. This is a mechanical-fidelity choice, not a leniency choice — a per-sentence split would
   diverge further from what the actual extractor operates over, not closer to it.
2. **`**Confidence:** [...]` lines are out-of-scope for tracing, under both readings.** The output
   template (`shared/spine/references/output-template.md` §6) requires this field independently of
   the closure-ledger contract, with its own D-07 obligation ("name the specific `GT-N?` inputs...
   that caused the downgrade"). A confidence caveat is not itself a fresh assertion resting on §4 in
   the sense D-03(a) polices; it is a disclosure field. This call is applied uniformly and does not
   change the outcome for any document where the Confidence line's own chain-id citations would have
   traced it anyway (Q-N1, Q-N5) — it only removes an already-ambiguous field from judgment rather
   than manufacturing either verdict.
3. **A newly observed, undisclosed citation-family mismatch (distinct from FIX-CONTRACT-01's three
   fixed limitations).** One document (`Q-N4`) cites Assumptions-Table ids (`A-2`, `A-9`, `A-15`) in
   its `6. Conclusion` section in places a chain id would be expected. An `A-N` token is not a chain
   identifier (criterion 1) and is not a `GT-N` identifier (criterion 2) — under the strict text of
   adapted D-03(a), an assumption-id citation satisfies **none** of the three recognized evidence
   forms, regardless of whether the underlying number is in fact chain-derived. This is recorded as
   a genuinely new observation for the record (a fourth citation-family mismatch, not the bare-letter
   /abbreviation/scope-filter patterns FIX-CONTRACT-01 already fixed), not folded into the existing
   three.
