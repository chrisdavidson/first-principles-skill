## Finding 1 — no detectable quality difference

The arms are indistinguishable on every aggregate: same pass rate, same band total, same mean.
The **failures track the problem, not the arm** — P2 (churn) failed in *both* arms; P1 and P3
passed in both. Problem difficulty dominates; the 22-line difference is not visible.

**Honest limits.** N=3, single run per cell, single judge per document, 4-level coarse scale — an
exact 35/35 tie is partly luck, and the correct claim is *"no detectable difference,"* not
*"proven identical."* The contrast is only 3.6%, so this **cannot** support the general claim that
body size never matters. It supports the specific claim that **this** compression cost nothing
measurable.

**What that means for v8.6:** compression was a cost optimisation, and it did not damage quality.
That is a genuine, if modest, success — v8.6 removed 22 lines and bought no quality debt.

**What it means for the optimisation axis:** the axis was never justified on quality grounds and
still isn't. Both arms sit at 2/3 PASS. Shaving 22 more lines will not move that number; the
failures are structural and identical across arms.

## Finding 2 — the rubric discriminates, but on a 2-level scale

It works: it separated 6 documents into 4 PASS / 2 FAIL on reproducible, quoted structural
evidence, and the **hand-wavy cap was the operative discriminator** in both failures (neither was
gated by an Absent). Judges applied the precedence rule correctly, declining to double-count one
defect across criteria.

But its usable range is narrow. Across 36 scored cells:

| Band | Count |
|---|---|
| Rigorous | 7 (6 of them C1) |
| Sound | 20 |
| Hand-wavy | 9 |
| Absent | **0** |

- **C1 was Rigorous in all 6** — no discrimination. Essence-framing is a solved problem.
- **C3 was Sound in all 6** — no discrimination. Every analysis cites GT sources by *category*
  ("published benchmarks", "physical law") rather than specific source, which caps C3 at Sound
  permanently.
- **Absent was never awarded.** The gate never fired.

So 2 of 6 criteria are constants and the effective scale is Sound-vs-Hand-wavy. The instrument
has real but limited resolution — adequate for pass/fail, too coarse to detect a 3.6% change.

## Finding 3 — four reproducible methodology defects (the most valuable output)

Independent judges, both arms, all three problems converged on the same structural failures.
These are invisible to the Step 0 marker detectors, which count phrases and never check whether
chains cover conclusions.

1. **The `Verdict` column never uses the prescribed vocabulary.** The rubric requires
   Accept / Challenge / Discard. Every analysis writes outcome statements instead — "True,
   magnitude workload-dependent", "Rejected by default", "Probably false as sole cause". Flagged
   in all 3 C2 Hand-wavy bands. A spec-vs-behaviour mismatch, 6/6.
2. **The `[Assumes: X]` inline token is essentially never emitted.** Judges surfaced 7–15
   undeclared chain-step assumptions per document. Only one document (Q4) used the token at all.
3. **Conclusions appear in section 6 with no chain in section 4.** Flagged in all six documents.
   The single most consistent defect in the corpus.
4. **Trade-off matrices and second-order effect lists are presented as "chains"** without the
   prescribed `GT-N + GT-M → [intermediate] → [conclusion]` form. Flagged in 4.

Defects 1 and 2 are cheap prompt-level fixes in the output contract. Defect 3 is the substantive
one and is what a quality milestone should target.

## Methodology warnings (cost 2 near-misses)

1. **The orchestrator summarises the analysis by ~85%.** Agent `tool_result` = 16,866 chars;
   `result` field = 2,454. Scoring the `result` field — the obvious choice — would have scored
   summaries. Independently corroborates the v8.5 S-P02 diagnosis.
2. **Multiple `tool_result` blocks per run.** Two B-arm runs contained orchestrator `Read` calls,
   one of which read **`validation-rubric.md` itself**. Naive concatenation glued the rubric onto
   the analysis. Uncaught, this would have inflated arm B and produced a confident, false
   "compression hurt quality" verdict. Fixed by matching `tool_result.tool_use_id` to the Agent
   `tool_use.id`.

Two extraction traps, each capable of fabricating a decisive result, in *opposite* directions.
This is directly relevant to how much weight the project's existing live measurements deserve.

## Recommended next steps

- **Do not run another compression milestone on quality grounds.** No quality headroom is being
  lost or gained; both arms are 2/3.
- **Target defect 3** (untraced conclusions) — reproducible, structural, and the one defect that
  actually degrades the artifact's core promise that every conclusion traces to a ground truth.
- **Fix defects 1–2 in the output contract** — near-free, and they would lift C2 off Hand-wavy in
  half the corpus.
- **If the rubric is to be a measurement instrument**, either accept its 2-level range or add
  discrimination at C1/C3.
